The case for the price. The market is pricing a $99.8M-market-cap commercial mortgage REIT that has just doubled its share count to buy its own external manager, in a CRE credit environment that is still working through 2021-2022 vintage bridge loans. On 2026-08-06 ACRES completed the all-stock acquisition of ACRES Capital Corp., issuing 7,478,994 shares (a net increase of roughly 6.3M after eliminating ACR shares held by ACC), alongside a $200M 8.625% senior secured notes placement due 2031 used partly to retire $150M of 5.75% unsecured notes maturing in August 2026. Refinancing unsecured 5.75% paper with secured 8.625% paper is what a lender does when the unsecured market is closed to it, and the coupon step-up is roughly $4.3M a year of incremental interest against a company whose Q2 net interest income was $10.5M. Q2 2026 was a GAAP net loss of $12.5M, $(1.87) per share, including about $9.1M of internalization costs, and GAAP book value fell 10.7% in one quarter to $26.76 from $29.98. On 2026-08-12 the company sold a further 2.22M Series C preferred shares at $23.75 for roughly $52.8M, adding to a capital stack that was already preferred-heavy. A stock down 30% over 63 days on $581K of average daily dollar volume is the market marking a levered, preferred-heavy CRE lender whose common sits behind a growing preferred stack, not an oversight.

Data quality. The screen raised NO flags on this name and at least three were warranted. FIRST, and decisively: valuation.justified_pb computes tangible common equity as equity_series[0] minus goodwill minus intangibles, and never deducts non-controlling interests or preferred stock, although both are captured in the same fundamentals record and are correctly subtracted in enterprise_value. For ACR the FY2025 equity of $420.796M includes $129.796M of non-controlling interest and the carrying value of the Series C and Series D preferred; the extract separately records preferred as $0.0 because PreferredStockValue picks up par, not liquidation preference. The company's own reported GAAP book value per share was $26.76 at 6/30/2026, against the screen's $58.37. SECOND, the share count: 7,131,101 as of 2026-08-03 is a pre-merger figure, filed three days before a merger that issued 7,478,994 shares for a net increase of about 6.3M. The correct current count is roughly 13.4M, so market capitalisation is about $188M, not $99.8M. Combined, tangible book per share is overstated by roughly 4x and the entire +237.5% gap is manufactured. Corrected, ACR trades at approximately 1.0x tangible common book. THIRD, the 7.0% cost of equity comes from a beta of 0.4 with R-squared 0.053 on a stock with $581K of daily volume, while the company's own secured debt prices at 8.625%. The non-controlling-interest and preferred defect is NOT specific to ACR — it applies to every one of the 338 names in the Financials cohort that carries either, and it systematically overstates cheapness. I resolved none of this by reading a filing: SEC egress is blocked from this runtime, so the merger terms, the Q2 book value and the preferred issuance are all from secondary sources, and the post-merger balance sheet is inferred. The arithmetic defect in justified_pb, however, is verifiable directly from the committed extract and the source code and does not depend on any of them.
