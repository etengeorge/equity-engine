The case for the price. The market pays 1.72x tangible book for an Arkansas bank that has just finished repositioning its balance sheet and is now earning an adjusted 15.5% return on tangible common equity with a net interest margin expanding 8bp to 3.92%. The price is a bet that the clean-up is behind it: a $562.8M securities-repositioning loss taken in Q3 2025 swapped a low-yielding bond book for current coupons, and a branch and headcount restructuring announced in September 2026 is taking the cost base down. At 1.72x tangible book against a 15.5% adjusted return and a 9.5% cost of equity, the market is paying roughly fair value for a bank that has done the painful thing and is now earning its keep — and discounting the fact that roughly 39% of its equity is goodwill.

What changed. Nothing material since the Q3 print, but the engine's reading of the history is wrong by 16.8 percentage points of return and that is the whole of its -125.3% gap. The engine's 'sustainable ROTCE' of -1.3% is the mean of a three-year GAAP window whose newest observation is a FY2025 net LOSS of $397.6M. That loss is not operating: Q3 2025 alone was a net loss of $562,792k, and it is a securities-portfolio repositioning — tangible book per share was $13.45 at Q3 2025 and is $13.52 today, essentially unchanged across the year in which the loss was taken, because the unrealised loss already sat in accumulated other comprehensive income. Goodwill is flat at $1,320,799k across all three years, so there was no impairment. The company's own Q3 2026 figures: net income $47.1M, ROAA 0.77%, return on average common equity 5.37%, adjusted ROAA 1.29% and ADJUSTED ROTCE 15.50%. Nine-month 2026 net income is $182.3M against a nine-month 2025 LOSS of $475.6M.
