The case for the price. At $6.13 and a $6.1B market capitalisation the market is pricing a pre-commercial aircraft manufacturer as a call option on FAA type certification. The bull case is entirely forward: Joby is in the final stage of type certification, has begun real-world air taxi flights in Texas under the FAA's Project Nexus eIPP, is preparing commercial service in Dubai, has demonstrated autonomous flight on a cross-country tour with the J208, and has acquired Blade to own distribution. None of that produces revenue today, which is precisely the point - the buyer is underwriting a binary regulatory outcome with a very large payoff, not a cash flow stream. The stock is down 56% over twelve months because that option's time value is decaying while certification slips.

What changed. The whole sector has de-rated - Joby, Archer and EHang are all near 52-week lows with no single catalyst - and Joby is down about 53-54% year to date. Operationally: Texas eIPP commercial-route flights began in September 2026, a 10-state J208 autonomous tour is underway targeting freight, medical, emergency and defense applications, and Dubai commercial preparation continues. On 2026-08-11 an 8-K under items 1.01, 3.02, 7.01 and 8.01 reported an unregistered sale of equity. On 2026-09-11 Joby filed a resale prospectus supplement covering up to 2.42 million earn-out shares tied to the Blade acquisition. An 8-K under item 5.02 on 2026-09-09 reported an officer or director change. At 2026-06-30 the balance sheet carries cash and cash equivalents of $629,861 thousand plus short-term investments of $1,633,965 thousand - $2,263,826 thousand of total liquidity, up from $1,407,916 thousand at the prior year end. First-half 2026 net cash used in operating activities was $317,582 thousand against $217,533 thousand a year earlier, and the first-half net loss was $355,393 thousand.

Base case. There is no defensible base case and supplying one would be dishonest. Joby has no meaningful revenue, negative EBITDA, and no cash-flow history the model can use - normalized FCFF is not merely negative, it is unavailable, computed over zero years. The value of this company is a probability-weighted terminal outcome conditional on FAA type certification, and neither a discounted cash flow nor a trailing multiple can express that. The correct output is no_model.
