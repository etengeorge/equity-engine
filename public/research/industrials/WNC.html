The case for the price. At $11.71 Wabash is priced as a trailer maker in the third year of a freight depression whose current earnings are negative and whose balance sheet has just been refinanced on lender-imposed terms. Q2 2026 gross margin was 3.7% and H1 was 0.66%; H1 adjusted EBITDA was NEGATIVE $47.8M and H1 free cash flow negative $34.2M; Q3 is guided to a -4.0% operating margin and $(0.45) EPS. A $476M equity value on ~$665M of gross debt against a company burning cash is a levered option on the cycle turning, and that is what the market is pricing.

Devil's advocate.

  • Strongest counter: The refusal is the error, because a trough base is exactly when a cyclical is cheap. Wabash earned $221.5M of free cash flow in 2023 against today's $476M market cap; if the cycle merely normalises to a mid-cycle $120-150M the equity is worth a multiple of the price, and every recovery signal in the September deck is dated and third-party sourced (ACT orders +195% y/y, backlog +14% sequentially, tender rejections 14% vs 5%).
