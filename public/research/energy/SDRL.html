The case for the price. At $39.82 the market is paying $2.7bn of enterprise value for a deepwater drilling franchise with $3.3bn of contract backlog — roughly two years of revenue — more than 85% of available drillship days contracted for 2027, $800M of new awards and extensions secured in three months, net debt of only $244M against guided adjusted EBITDA of $440-460M, and a buyback running. The bull case is a cycle call with contracts behind it: 2025 was the trough (operating cash flow of negative $28M), 2026 EBITDA guidance has been raised twice, the West Carina award of ~$420M completes the third and final repricing of three legacy contracts with the improved dayrate effective in 2027, and deepwater demand is tightening while supply is fixed. On that view the current year understates earning power and 2027-28 is materially better.
