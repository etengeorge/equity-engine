The case for the price. Freshworks is a $965M-revenue service-operations software company growing 14-15% with a 26-30% adjusted free-cash-flow margin, and the market is paying about 10x enterprise value to free cash flow for it. On the true share count of 262.3 million, enterprise value is roughly $2.72bn against FY2026 free cash flow of about $255-265M. The market's case for requiring only around zero five-year growth is the shape of the growth: the number of customers contributing more than $5,000 of ARR grew just 5% year over year, while the $100,000-plus cohort grew 25%. So the SMB base that Freshworks was built on is essentially flat and all the growth is a land-up-market motion in Employee Experience, which is now 59% of recurring revenue. That is a harder, more competitive, longer-sales-cycle business against ServiceNow and Zendesk, and the market is pricing a decelerating curve (34%, 20%, 16%, 15% over four years) into an AI-disrupted category. It is also paying for a share count being shrunk with $387M of nine-month buybacks against only $198.6M of nine-month operating cash flow.
