The case for the price. The market is paying $4.2bn for a drone and counter-drone platform being assembled in real time out of Israeli and European defence technology, in a year when European and Middle Eastern autonomous-defence budgets are being rewritten. Ondas is buying the components rather than renting them: World View in April, an item 2.01 acquisition on 2026-08-10, Gate Technologies (Israel) and Bron Technologies (Poland) closed 2026-09-14, and CaribouLabs, Insignito Solutions and Ottopia Technologies closed 2026-09-23 - the last of these announced the day before this screen priced the stock. A $205M precision-strike contract was reported on 2026-09-14. At $647.3M of average daily dollar volume on a $4.2bn company, the tape is not valuing cash flows; it is trading a consolidation story.

What changed. Essentially the entire company, inside six weeks, and mostly in stock. The 2026-09-14 8-K discloses the acquisition of 100% of Gate and Bron for $105.0M in cash plus 10,689,655 Consideration Shares, plus a $25M working-capital adjustment, plus approximately $22.5M to be issued within nine months, plus contingent earn-out payments of up to $185,000,000 over two years PAYABLE IN COMMON STOCK. The 2026-09-23 8-K covers the resale of a further 7,821,445 shares issued on that date for the CaribouLabs, Insignito and Ottopia acquisitions. That is roughly 18.5 million shares issued as acquisition consideration in ten days, before the earn-out.
