Base case. I assume 5% FCFF growth over five years, and I take that from the company's own long-range plan rather than from its recent revenue line. InterDigital's stated 2030 targets, set in September 2024 and reaffirmed since, are more than $1B of annual RECURRING revenue and $600M of adjusted EBITDA on TOTAL revenue of more than $800M. That last number is the one that matters and it is easy to miss: 2026 guidance already puts total revenue at $810M at the midpoint, so management's own 2030 plan implies total revenue roughly flat for four years, with the growth in ARR ($626M to $1B) being offset by the run-off of catch-up and one-time settlement revenue.
