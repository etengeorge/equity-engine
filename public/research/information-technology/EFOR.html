The case for the price. Everforth — ASGN until it rebranded on 2026-04-24 — is the fourth-largest IT staffing firm in the US and the market is pricing the AI disruption of exactly that business. The company runs seven brands (Apex Systems, Creative Circle, CyberCoders, ECS, GlideFast, TopBloc, Quinnox) across a 70% commercial and 30% federal split. The bear case the price expresses is straightforward: contract IT labour is the most directly substitutable input in the enterprise technology stack, generative AI is substituting for it now, and the federal side is shrinking too — new contract awards of $0.9B on a trailing-twelve-month book-to-bill of 0.8 to 1. Against $1.5 billion of debt and a $1.3B market cap, a business whose revenue does not grow and whose margin is compressing is a levered stub. Q2 revenue was down 1.3% year over year with adjusted EBITDA margin at 9.6% against 10.6%, and free cash flow for the first half halved. A -18.6% five-year FCFF path is what the market thinks a shrinking, levered staffing book is worth.

What changed. Q2 2026 (2026-07-29) beat the high end of guidance on revenue, net income, adjusted EBITDA and margin — but the underlying numbers are worse than the headline. Revenue $1,007.0M against $1,020.6M, down 1.3%; Commercial $701.7M against $708.1M with four of five industries declining and only TMT up (+$7.7M, +5.6%); Federal $305.3M against $312.5M.
