The case for the price. At $45.98 the market requires roughly -5% to +1% five-year FCFF growth once the enterprise value is corrected for the Domo purchase and the discount rate is taken off a failed regression - and that is close to what Progress actually delivers. Third-quarter revenue FELL 2% year over year to $246.0 million and annualised recurring revenue grew 1% to $873 million, so the market is pricing a business that is flat at best. Against that it is a formidable cash machine: 43% non-GAAP operating margins, guided fiscal 2026 unlevered free cash flow of $330-338 million, a basic share count down 4.5% year over year, and debt reduced from $1,400.3 million to $1,232.6 million. The bear and the bull are both in the price: flat organic, high cash conversion, rising leverage.
