2026-09-03 — NO_MODEL (conviction: low)

The case for the price. Riot is no longer a bitcoin miner being valued as one. On 10 Aug 2026 it disclosed a 20-year agreement to supply 191MW of IT capacity at Rockdale to a frontier AI company subsequently identified as Anthropic, expected to generate approximately $9.1 billion through June 2048 with extension options taking the total toward $16.1 billion — roughly $400M+ of annual contracted rent from a single lease. On the same day it disclosed a non-binding letter of intent covering its 1GW Corsicana site, which management said could produce more than $1 billion of annual rent once fully deployed. Against a $7.0B market capitalization, one signed 20-year contract plus one LOI plausibly covers the entire equity value on contracted revenue alone. The Engineering segment tripled year on year to $37.3M with gross margin going from ~7% to over 27% and a $177.1M backlog, so Riot can build what it leases. That is why Morgan Stanley lists Riot among preferred operators for the powered-shell deal wave and why KBW carries a $35 target. The buyer at $18.64 is buying secured power at scale — the genuinely scarce input in AI infrastructure — not hashrate.

What changed. Q2 2026 (10 Aug): revenue $174.2M, +14% year on year, but a net loss of $237.2M and diluted EPS of $(0.68), driven by more than $240M of non-cash items including a $75M mark-to-market loss on bitcoin holdings and $98M of depreciation and amortization. Bitcoin production of 1,587 BTC against 1,426 a year earlier at a deployed hashrate of 44.4 EH/s (~4.6% of network), with a direct cost to mine of $49,912 per BTC and a net power cost of $0.036/kWh after $10M of curtailment credits. Riot sold 9,665 BTC in H1 2026 to fund the AI infrastructure build. Data centre revenue was $23.2M ($4.9M operating lease, $18.3M tenant fit-out). After the quarter closed: the 191MW / $9.1B Anthropic lease at Rockdale, and the non-binding Corsicana LOI. A 14 Aug 8-K carried items 1.01 and 2.03 (a material agreement and a new debt obligation). Reports in early September suggest pledged bitcoin collateral may be released, freeing capital for the data centre build. The stock is -9.6% over 5 days, -13.6% over 21 days and -32.6% over 63 days even as the lease news landed.

Base case. I decline to state a growth rate, and that refusal is the substantive answer rather than an evasion. Riot's normalized FCFF base is not merely low, it is unavailable — the engine reports 'mean of CFO minus capex over 0y' and flags nonpositive normalized FCFF, negative EBITDA and a negative free cash flow year in the window. There is no cash flow stream to discount. More importantly, the cash flow stream that will exist is not a projection of the one that did: the business is mid-transformation from mining bitcoin to leasing power and shells, and those have entirely different revenue recognition, capital intensity, counterparty risk and duration. Applying any growth rate to a mining cash flow base would be pricing the wrong company, and inventing one for the leasing business would be fabricating a fair value for a name the model has correctly declined to value. The honest output is that a free-cash-flow DCF cannot value Riot today.

Devil's advocate.

  • Strongest counter: The counter to my refusal is that no_model is a cop-out when the brief hands me a multiples table.   • What would prove it: A definitive, executed Corsicana lease with disclosed megawatts, term and annual rent — that single document converts Riot from unvaluable to valuable, because contracted rent over a stated term is exactly the kind of stream a DCF handles well. Absent that, an annualized run-rate for the data centre segment once Rockdale capacity is energized would let the leasing business be valued separately from the mining business.
  • Already visible today: The counter loses, and the multiples table is the reason why rather than an argument for it. Note that the two multiples disagree by a factor of nearly three ($10.12 blended versus $27.34 on tangible book), and that the ev_gross_profit row returns $-1.00 at every percentile because gross profit is negative. A comparables number computed on the pre-transformation revenue base is not evidence about the post-transformation company. Reporting -45.7% as though it were a valuation would be exactly the error the standing lessons warn against.

Any of those could dominate the equity value.
