The case for the price. J.Jill is a 255-store women's apparel retailer with $600M or so of annual sales, about 47% of it direct-to-consumer, carrying $71.2M of debt against a $325M market capitalisation. At $21.74 the market is pricing FCFF to decline. The case for that is on the face of the company's own numbers once the one-time items come out: year-to-date net sales are DOWN 2.7% and year-to-date comparable sales are DOWN 4.2%; SG&A has risen 320bp as a share of sales on eight net new stores, lease renewals, marketing and shipping; and full-year comparable sales are guided at down 1% to up 1%. This is a mature specialty brand spending more to hold its customer file flat, with 30% of its enterprise value in operating leases that are kept as opex.

What changed. Q2 fiscal 2026 was reported 2026-09-09 with the 10-Q, and the stock rose 12% in five days on 3.2x average volume against a headline beat and a raised full-year outlook. The quarter was almost entirely a tariff refund. Net sales $154.8M, up 0.5%; comparable sales up 0.5%. GROSS MARGIN WAS 76.8% AGAINST 68.4%, UP 840bp — AND THE COMPANY DISCLOSES THAT EXCLUDING $13.3M OF NET IEEPA TARIFF REFUNDS IT WAS 68.3%, i.e. DOWN 10bp YEAR OVER YEAR. Operating income rose $7.5M to $24.3M while the refund alone was $13.3M, so ex-refund operating income fell from $16.8M to roughly $11.0M. J.Jill gives the cleanest version itself: 'Adjusted EBITDA for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 excluding net tariff refunds and deployment of these refunds toward strategic initiatives and elevated fuel costs during the period was $20.1 million' against $25.6M a year earlier — DOWN 21%. The raised full-year outlook 'includes net tariff refunds as well as the deployment of these refunds toward investments primarily focused on marketing', so the windfall is being spent, not banked. Full-year guidance: net sales flat to +2%, comparable sales -1% to +1%. Q3: net sales +3-5%, comps +1-3%, gross margin about flat, adjusted EBITDA $20.0-22.0M. This is the third name in today's ten carrying an IEEPA refund, alongside SIG and BBW.
