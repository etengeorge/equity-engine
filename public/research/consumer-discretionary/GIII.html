The case for the price. G-III is losing its two largest licences on a known schedule and buying its way into owned brands. Fiscal 2027 net sales are guided to approximately $2.71bn against $2.96bn, a decline that 'incorporates the loss of approximately $460 million of sales from Calvin Klein and Tommy Hilfiger products', and more follows. Adjusted EBITDA is guided DOWN to $174-178M from $192.4M and non-GAAP EPS DOWN to $2.20-2.30 from $2.61. On 2026-09-01 the company closed an approximately $500 million investment in the Marc Jacobs business from LVMH, alongside WHP Global, which it says will be 'slightly dilutive in fiscal 2027' and dilutive for the first twelve months. So the market is pricing a business whose revenue base is contracting by design, whose margin is being rebuilt through acquisition rather than organic growth, and which has just spent roughly a third of its market capitalisation on a brand it does not own the IP to - it licenses it from a joint venture. A price that requires deeply negative FCFF growth is the market doing exactly the right arithmetic on a dated contractual cliff.

What changed. The acquisition closed AFTER the balance sheet the model uses, and that is the whole defect. The 2026-09-02 8-K carries items 1.01 and 2.01: the Marc Jacobs transaction completed 2026-09-01, one month after the 2026-07-31 balance sheet, with G-III funding 'its approximately $500 million investment using cash on hand and borrowings'. Structurally, IPCo (co-owned with WHP Global) retained the Marc Jacobs intellectual property and G-III took the operating business plus a licence to use the brands in the US, Canada, Mexico and Western Europe. Also new and large: the IEEPA tariff refund, in two places at once. The six months to 2026-07-31 carry a $126.4 million reduction in cost of goods sold from recovery of previously incurred IEEPA tariffs on inventory sold in the PRIOR year, plus $4.2 million of interest income on the refund - so of $178.7M of six-month operating cash flow, roughly $130.6M is the refund, and ex-refund H1 CFO was about $48M against $168.9M a year earlier. That is why GAAP guidance shows net income tripling to $181-185M ($4.10-4.20) while non-GAAP net income FALLS to $97-101M ($2.20-2.30). Q3 fiscal 2027 sales are guided to approximately $870.0M against $988.6M.
