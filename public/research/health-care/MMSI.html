The case for the price. Merit Medical is a well-run medical device manufacturer growing organic constant-currency revenue at 9%, raising guidance twice this year, and expanding non-GAAP operating margin 140bp with non-GAAP EPS up 18%. At $87.19 the market caps it at $5.2B, an enterprise value of $5.5B — about 3.4x guided fiscal 2026 revenue of $1.631-1.643B. That is an unremarkable multiple for medical technology, and the strongest case for the price is that a durable 8-9% organic grower with visible margin expansion and a diversified catheter and device portfolio deserves it. The market is not paying a bubble multiple; it is paying a quality multiple for a company that has been delivering.

What changed. Q2 2026 was reported 2026-07-30. Revenue $418.8M, up 10% reported and 9% organic constant currency, with US sales up 10% and results above the high end of management's own expectations. Full-year guidance was RAISED on both lines: revenue to $1.631-1.643B from $1.612-1.634B, non-GAAP EPS to $4.25-4.35 from $4.01-4.15, which is 11-14% growth against a prior 5-8%. Non-GAAP operating margin improved 140bp and non-GAAP EPS rose 18%.
