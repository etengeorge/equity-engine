The case for the price. The market's case is that this is a defensive, fragmented, GDP-plus end market with a genuine consolidator at the front of it: Q2 revenue rose 10.0% to $606.0 million, patient visits per day 2.6%, and adjusted EBITDA 22.5% to $140.9 million as the fixed-cost base levered.
