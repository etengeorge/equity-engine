The case for the price. Definium Therapeutics is a late-stage clinical biopharmaceutical company with no approved product and no revenue, developing DT120 — a proprietary orally disintegrating tablet formulation of lysergide (LSD) — for generalized anxiety disorder, major depressive disorder and PTSD. The $5.1B market capitalisation at $37.99, after a 304.6% twelve-month gain, is a probability-weighted claim on that single molecule. The case for it is unusually concrete for a clinical-stage name: two positive Phase 3 readouts in 2026 (Voyage in GAD in August, Emerge in MDD, the latter showing a statistically significant 8.1-point placebo-adjusted MADRS improvement at week 6 with durable efficacy through week 12, p<0.0001), two FDA Breakthrough Therapy designations — GAD first and MDD granted 2026-09-08 — and $1.1B of cash, cash equivalents and investments at 2026-06-30 funding operations into 2030.

On 2026-09-08 the FDA granted Breakthrough Therapy designation for DT120 in MDD following positive Phase 3 Emerge results, the second such designation for the programme.
