The case for the price. Praxis owns the first pharmacological programme ever to succeed in Phase 3 in essential tremor. In October 2025 both pivotal Essential3 studies of ulixacaltamide met their primary endpoints, and the stock rose 231% in a day to $190. Essential tremor is a large, chronically undertreated neurological indication with no drug approved specifically for it — patients are managed off-label with propranolol and primidone, or with focused ultrasound. Ulixacaltamide has Breakthrough Therapy designation, the NDA was accepted in April 2026 with a PDUFA date of 2027-01-29, and the FDA is not planning an advisory committee. Behind it, relutrigine for SCN2A and SCN8A developmental and epileptic encephalopathies is under priority review with a PDUFA date of 2026-12-27, and the mid-cycle meeting produced no major safety or efficacy concerns and no advisory committee. The company held approximately $1.4B of cash and investments at 30 June 2026 with runway stated into 2028, so it can reach both decisions and launch without financing. A $9.6B market capitalisation is the market capitalising a first-in-class launch into a very large indication, four months from approval.

What changed. A great deal, in both directions. Positive: FDA accepted the ulixacaltamide NDA in April 2026 with a 2027-01-29 PDUFA date and no advisory committee planned; expanded Essential3 analyses were presented at AAN in April 2026; the relutrigine mid-cycle review surfaced no major safety or efficacy concerns. Negative: in June 2026 the POWER1 Phase 2/3 study of vormatrigine in highly refractory focal-onset seizures MISSED its primary endpoint of reduction in monthly focal seizure frequency at week 12, though it met a secondary endpoint on the proportion of patients achieving a 50% or greater reduction — management attributes the miss to dose, a six-week duration that was too short, and entry criteria weighted to heavily pre-treated patients, and plans to amend and restart POWER2 and POWER3 by Q4 2026. Also negative: the FDA deemed additional sensitivity analyses submitted for relutrigine a MAJOR AMENDMENT and extended the review, moving the PDUFA date from 2026-09-27 to 2026-12-27. Q2 2026 operating expenses were $96.9M against $76.0M a year earlier. The stock is -8.3% over five sessions and roughly flat over 63, after +634.8% over 252.

A first-ever positive Phase 3 establishes efficacy; it does not establish pricing power or that neurologists will switch.
