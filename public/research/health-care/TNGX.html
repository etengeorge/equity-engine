The case for the price. Tango Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company with no product revenue, and at $22.53 and a $3.8bn market capitalisation the market is pricing one hypothesis: that vopimetostat, its PRMT5 inhibitor, works in combination with Revolution Medicines' RAS(ON) inhibitors in MTAP-deleted, RAS-mutant cancers. On 2026-10-23 at the ESMO Congress in Madrid the company reported what it called promising Phase 1/2 results for that combination in metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma and non-small cell lung cancer, and said a pivotal study in front-line MTAP-deleted pancreatic cancer is expected to begin in the first half of 2027. MTAP deletion is a synthetic-lethal target with a genuinely large addressable population across pancreatic and lung cancer, pancreatic cancer has almost no effective front-line options, and a combination that pairs a first-in-class PRMT5 inhibitor with the RAS(ON) class — the most important new mechanism in oncology — is a credible way to build a franchise. The stock is up 189.2% over twelve months on that thesis. It is also down 19.3% over the last five days and 17.1% over 63, which is the market's own verdict on the ESMO data: the word used in the release was 'promising', and the tape read it as less than that.

What changed. The 2026-10-23 8-K under items 7.01 and 8.01, carrying both a press release (EX-99.1) and an investor presentation (EX-99.2), is the event behind the 19.3% five-day fall and it postdates everything in the model. Content: Phase 1/2 results for vopimetostat combined with Revolution Medicines' RAS(ON) inhibitors in MTAP-deleted and RAS-mutant metastatic PDAC and NSCLC, presented at ESMO, with an investor webcast focused on pancreatic cancer held the same morning, and a pivotal front-line MTAP-deleted pancreatic study guided to initiate in the first half of 2027. The second new fact, from the 2026-08-11 Q2 release, is financial and it changes how the brief's own multiples should be read: COLLABORATION REVENUE WAS $0 for the three months to 2026-06-30, against about $3M a year earlier, following the truncation of the prior collaboration. Earlier filings in the window — a 2026-06-10 item 1.01, 2026-06-23 and 2026-08-06 item 5.02 officer changes, and a 2026-06-08 item 7.01/8.01 — are not material to a valuation.
