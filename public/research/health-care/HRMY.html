Devil's advocate.

  • Strongest counter: The market is too pessimistic about a specialty franchise with a durable moat. Narcolepsy is a small, physician-concentrated market; generic entrants into it have historically been few and erosion slower than in primary care. Harmony has $600M of net cash and a demonstrated record of buying assets (Zynerba, Epygenix), so the terminal value is not a stub, it is a platform. On that reading -11.9% is too harsh and the true number is closer to my bull case.

  • Already visible today: Only partly, and it is thin. The company describes 'potential protection of the franchise through other formulations into the 2040s via additional patents/applications' - a statement of possibility, not of a filed programme with a date. EPX-100 is at the encore-data stage. I found no dated regulatory milestone that lands before 2030.
