The case for the price. Immunovant is a clinical-stage antibody company whose value is entirely the probability-weighted commercial outcome of IMVT-1402, a subcutaneous anti-FcRn antibody, led by Graves' disease. There is no revenue, no free cash flow and no earnings; the $5.7bn market capitalisation is a bet on a pipeline. The market's case is specific: IMVT-1402 is a next-generation asset in a mechanism (FcRn blockade) already commercially validated by argenx's efgartigimod, Graves' disease is a large under-served indication with no approved targeted therapy, and management states the $797.8M of cash at 2026-06-30 provides 'runway to the potential commercial launch of IMVT-1402 in GD'. That is a coherent thing to underwrite. It is not a thing a discounted-cash-flow model can price.
