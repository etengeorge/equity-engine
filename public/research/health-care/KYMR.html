The case for the price. Kymera is a $9.9B clinical-stage biotech with $1.5B of cash and no product revenue, and the price is a probability-weighted bet on one molecule. KT-621 is a first-in-class oral degrader of STAT6, the transcription factor carrying IL-4/IL-13 signalling — the same pathway Dupixent addresses by injection. An oral drug that works on that pathway addresses a market measured in tens of billions, and that is why the stock is up 189% in twelve months and 67% in three. Enrollment in the BROADEN2 Phase 2b atopic dermatitis trial completed nearly six months ahead of schedule with topline data due by year-end 2026, Phase 3 in AD is planned for mid-2027, and the BREADTH Phase 2b asthma trial reads out in late 2027. Cash runway extends into 2029, so there is no financing cliff forcing a raise before the data. The price is not a cash-flow judgement; it is the market assigning a probability to KT-621 working.
