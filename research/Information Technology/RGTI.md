**The case for the price.** Rigetti is a $5.1 billion market capitalisation on roughly $8 million of trailing revenue and negative EBITDA, and the buyers know that. What they are paying for is optionality on superconducting quantum computing reaching commercial scale, and in the week before this screen they got two pieces of evidence for it: the U.S. Department of Commerce awarded Rigetti up to $100 million under the CHIPS Act to accelerate superconducting quantum research, and BofA Securities called quantum the 'next fire moment'. The federal government is now a shareholder — it took 7,739,938 shares as inducement for the award. IonQ raised full-year guidance to $450-460 million at its investor day and signed a $1.8 billion deal, which is the revenue scale the sector is arguing Rigetti eventually reaches. A holder is buying a call option on a technology with a national-security sponsor and a government balance sheet behind it, not a cash flow stream, and $377 million of daily dollar volume says the marginal buyer is trading that option rather than discounting anything.

**What changed.** The 8-K filed 2026-09-08 under Items 1.01, 3.02, 7.01 and 8.01, read in full. The Department covenants not to vote the shares except on changes to authorised shares or par value and on any business combination; there are resale shelf and piggyback registration rights; and if the Department terminates the agreement, Rigetti may repurchase the undisbursed portion of the shares for $1.00 in aggregate.
