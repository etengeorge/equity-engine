## 2026-11-20 — NO_MODEL (conviction: high)

- **Verdict:** no_model · price $33.90
- **Not repriced:** not a repriceable fcff name or no growth supplied

**The case for the price.** At $33.90, down 44% in 21 days, the market is pricing a company that has just agreed to buy two refineries for more than its own equity is worth and to fund it with up to $1.8bn of new stock and $2.25bn+ of new debt at 9.25%. The bull case the sellers of the paper are underwriting is the one in CVR's own lender deck: the $4.2bn price is ~3.6x the target's LTM EBITDA of $1,158M and ~4.2x an indicative mid-cycle EBITDA near $1.0bn, against comparable transformative refining transactions above 6.0x; pro forma LTM EBITDA is ~$1,907M against ~$749M standalone, a 155% increase; crude capacity goes from 207 to 384 mbpd, making CVI the fourth-largest PADD II refiner; and an integrated marketing platform with up to 590 branded locations adds roughly $4.50/bbl of margin uplift. The equity holder is being offered a levered call on Mid-Con cracks that the 2027 forward strip puts at $44.91-48.66 against ten-year averages of $28.50-30.38.

**What changed.** Everything that matters, and all of it after the 2026-09-30 balance sheet the model is built on. (1) CVR agreed to acquire the St. Paul Park, Minnesota and Mandan, North Dakota refineries and related marketing and logistics assets from subsidiaries of Marathon Petroleum and MPLX for a cash purchase price of $4,200 million, subject to adjustments, with reciprocal break fees of 2% ($84M) for antitrust failure and 6% ($252M) for willful breach, and an acquisition outside date of 2027-10-28 (8-K items 1.01/3.02, 2026-11-03). (2) An $800,000,000 Commercial Investor Equity Investment and an IEP Commitment of up to $500,000,000 from Icahn Enterprises, both at the Investment Price. (3) A $750M underwritten public offering announced 2026-11-03 — $500M primary from the company and $250M secondary from IEP Energy Holding, plus a $112.5M over-allotment on the secondary; completed follow-on equity of more than $1.2bn by 2026-11-09. (4) $1.25bn of 9.250% senior secured notes due 2032 priced 2026-11-19 at 98.980%, closing 2026-11-25, into escrow, plus a new $1.0bn senior secured term loan B and an ABL amendment raising commitments from $550M to $1.1bn. (5) The 5.750% Senior Notes due 2028 are to be redeemed. So the company is issuing up to $1.8bn of equity against a recorded $3.41bn market capitalisation and at least $2.25bn of new debt against a recorded $1.70bn of total debt.

**Base case.** No base case is admissible, because the three inputs the model needs are each about to change by more than the answer is worth. The FCFF base of $270.0M is standalone pre-acquisition CVR spanning a 2024 trough and an above-mid-cycle 2026, and it is also arithmetically wrong (see the data-quality note). The enterprise value of $4.00bn is computed from a 2026-09-30 balance sheet that contains none of the $4.2bn purchase price, none of the $2.25bn+ of new debt and none of the new equity. The share count of 100,530,599 predates every one of the three equity issuances. And `forward_dcf` derives equity value as enterprise value less the EXTRACT's net debt divided by the EXTRACT's share count, so any growth rate I supplied would be priced through a net-debt figure roughly $2.3bn too small and a count up to 50% too low — a per-share fair value wrong by a large multiple and wrong in the generous direction. This is the VAC and CXW condition: the unreachable term is the equity bridge, not the discount rate.

**Devil's advocate.**
- Strongest counter: That I am refusing on input hygiene when the economics are legible and, on the company's own filed numbers, attractive. The deck is a filed EX-99.1 with audited carve-out figures behind it: target LTM EBITDA $1,158M for $4.2bn is 3.6x, against stated comparable transactions above 6.0x, and the target's units ran at 94-99% utilisation with $8.27 of EBITDA per barrel against MPC's Mid-Con average of $5.78. If the deal closes at those multiples, a buyer at $33.90 — after a 44% fall — is buying the acquirer of an asset at well below the market-clearing price for refining assets, which is as close to a mechanical value transfer as this screen ever sees.
- What would prove it: A pro forma balance sheet and share count: the 8-K/A with ASC 805 supplemental pro forma disclosure, or the final offering documents showing how many shares the $800M Commercial Investor, the $500M IEP commitment and the $500M primary actually bought, and at what Investment Price.
- Already visible today: Partly, and what is visible weakens it. The multiple is only cheap against LTM EBITDA, and the deck itself labels the environment: 'Transformative Refining & Marketing System Acquisition at a Compelling Mid-Cycle Valuation in an ABOVE MID-CYCLE ENVIRONMENT'. CVI's own refining EBITDA went $138M (2024A) to $182M (2025A) to $694M (LTM) — a 3.8x move driven by cracks, not by the business — so the LTM denominator in every multiple quoted is a cyclical high. The financing terms are the market's own verdict and they are not supportive: SECURED paper at 9.250%, priced at 98.98, in a company management describes as carrying modest leverage. And two facts sit awkwardly together — the controlling stockholder is selling $250M (plus a $112.5M option) into the public offering while committing to buy up to $500M at the Investment Price. I cannot reconcile those into a clean read, and I record that rather than choosing the flattering interpretation. The counter does not fail on the merits; it fails because I cannot price it. The equity bridge is moving by more than the equity is worth and the schema cannot reach it.
- Left unresolved: The number of shares actually issued and the Investment Price, which fixes the dilution; the pro forma net debt at close; whether the acquisition clears antitrust before the 2027-10-28 outside date; and the size of the synergies, which the deck calls 'meaningful' and 'potential' without quantifying. Also unresolved: the degree to which the $1,278M of cash on the 2026-09-30 balance sheet is already earmarked, since the purchase agreement was signed after that date.

**Key risks.** A $4,200M cash acquisition larger than the recorded market capitalisation, funded with up to $1.8bn of new equity and $2.25bn+ of new debt including $1.25bn of 9.250% secured notes; Every valuation input — share count, enterprise value, cash-flow base — predates the transaction; the recorded +99.5% gap is not a measurement of anything; Pro forma leverage of ~2.0x is struck on an above-mid-cycle LTM EBITDA of $1.9bn; on the deck's own mid-cycle figures it is materially higher; The +12.8% five-year revenue CAGR baseline is measured from a $3,930M COVID-trough year against a revenue line that PEAKED at $10,896M and is now 34% below it; Icahn Enterprises is selling $250M (plus a $112.5M option) into the same offering in which it commits to buy up to $500M; Consolidated CVR Partners (37% owned) means a material non-controlling interest in the fertilizer segment sits inside the reported figures
**Watch for.** The 8-K/A with ASC 805 pro forma disclosure, and the final share count and Investment Price — the first point at which this name becomes modellable; Antitrust clearance, or a Burdensome Condition rejection triggering the $84M fee, before the 2027-10-28 outside date; Closing of the $1.25bn escrowed notes on 2026-11-25 and the $1.0bn TLB; a special mandatory redemption at 100% of issue price if the acquisition fails; Group 3 2-1-1 and 3-2-1 cracks against the ten-year averages of $28.50 and $30.38 — the whole thesis is the spread between the strip and that mean; Whether a restated `interest_expense` turns positive in the next extract, which alone lifts the FCFF base from $270M to roughly $414M

**Data quality.** THE FIFTH RECORDED INSTANCE OF THE PARENTHESISED NEGATIVE `interest_expense` BUG AND THE FIRST IN ENERGY ON A NAME THE MODEL ACTUALLY PRICES. The extract holds `interest_expense = -$52,000,000`, because the income statement presents 'Interest expense, net ( 21 ) ( 25 ) ( 105 ) ( 79 )' in parentheses and the extractor kept the sign — the same mechanism as AVNT, CARS, BDC and SLG, and the same presentation-parsing failure as SAH's negative capex. It reproduces to the dollar: mean(cfo-capex) over [-$41M, $225M, $743M] is $309M, and $309M + (-$52M x 0.75) = the recorded $270.0M. So `normalized_fcff` SUBTRACTS $39M where it should ADD about $105M (nine-month 2026 interest expense, net is $105M, roughly $140M annualised), and the corrected base is about $414M — 53% higher. Direction: a false SELL, so it partly offsets the EV and share-count errors, which run the other way. Every existing rate detector is defeated: |-52|/1,700 = 3.1%, inside the plausible band, so the ALTG >12% and PATK <2% tests both pass and only a sign test catches it. SECOND, the sign-crossing series: cfo-capex runs [-$41M, $225M, $743M], so min < 0 < max and neither cycle flag can compute a ratio — only `negative_fcf_year_in_window` and `lumpy_fcff_spread_2.5x_of_mean` fired, naming symptoms, exactly as the OSIS entry predicts. THIRD, the enterprise value of $4.00bn is correct as at 2026-09-30 and void thereafter, and the share count of 100,530,599 (dei, 2026-10-30) was accurate when taken and is about to be wrong by up to 50% — the MGY and ONDS shape at a larger scale. Because equity is derived as EV less the extract's net debt over the extract's count, no `final_growth` is admissible: a supplied number would print a per-share fair value far too high, in the same direction as a not-cheap lean, which is the NIC condition of an error that manufactures the verdict rather than colouring it.
