**The case for the price.** Amplify has simplified to two producing assets - the Beta field offshore southern California and the Bairoil CO2 flood in Wyoming - after divesting its non-core positions, and both are improving. Third-quarter production averaged 7.4 MBopd, 100% oil, up 9% sequentially and 15% from Q1, on new wells (C29 and C16, each about 370 gross Bopd with ten-month payouts and IRRs above 150%) plus royalty relief. Bairoil lease operating expense fell $3.0 million sequentially and $5.2 million year over year, with annualised savings now forecast above $20 million against 2025. Adjusted EBITDA was $10.8 million, up $2.2 million sequentially, despite $9.7 million of realised derivative losses, and the company says hedge prices are higher in 2027. It carries NO drawn debt and has bought back about 4.0% of its shares at a $4.35 average. At a $167 million market capitalisation against an improving two-asset base, holders are buying an operational turnaround.

**What changed.** Q3 results, 2026-11-09, and the stock rose 26.4% in five days. The genuinely new and dated item is ENHANCED BETA ROYALTY RELIEF: building on the End of Life relief secured in May 2026, Amplify obtained Special Case Royalty Relief at Beta effective 2026-11-01, taking the royalty rate on ALL Beta production to approximately 12.4%, down from 24.8% before any relief. The suspension threshold also rose to about $92 per BOE on a trailing twelve-month average from about $80. Unlike the prior framework, SCRR does not erode as volumes rise, so it improves drilling economics and extends field life. Also: no outstanding revolver debt at 2026-09-30, but liquidity of only $25.0 million ($10.0 million cash plus about $15.0 million of availability); a net LOSS of $18.9 million on derivative movements against net income of $17.3 million in Q2; operating cash flow of just $3.1 million in the quarter; and a material weakness still being remediated, named in the company's own risk language.
