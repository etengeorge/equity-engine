## 2026-09-16 — NO_MODEL (conviction: high)

- **Verdict:** no_model · price $6.13
- **Not repriced:** not a repriceable fcff name or no growth supplied

**The case for the price.** At $6.13 and a $6.1B market capitalisation the market is pricing a pre-commercial aircraft manufacturer as a call option on FAA type certification. The bull case is entirely forward: Joby is in the final stage of type certification, has begun real-world air taxi flights in Texas under the FAA's Project Nexus eIPP, is preparing commercial service in Dubai, has demonstrated autonomous flight on a cross-country tour with the J208, and has acquired Blade to own distribution. None of that produces revenue today, which is precisely the point - the buyer is underwriting a binary regulatory outcome with a very large payoff, not a cash flow stream. The stock is down 56% over twelve months because that option's time value is decaying while certification slips.

**What changed.** The whole sector has de-rated - Joby, Archer and EHang are all near 52-week lows with no single catalyst - and Joby is down about 53-54% year to date. Operationally: Texas eIPP commercial-route flights began in September 2026, a 10-state J208 autonomous tour is underway targeting freight, medical, emergency and defense applications, and Dubai commercial preparation continues. On 2026-08-11 an 8-K under items 1.01, 3.02, 7.01 and 8.01 reported an unregistered sale of equity. On 2026-09-11 Joby filed a resale prospectus supplement covering up to 2.42 million earn-out shares tied to the Blade acquisition. An 8-K under item 5.02 on 2026-09-09 reported an officer or director change. At 2026-06-30 the balance sheet carries cash and cash equivalents of $629,861 thousand plus short-term investments of $1,633,965 thousand - $2,263,826 thousand of total liquidity, up from $1,407,916 thousand at the prior year end. First-half 2026 net cash used in operating activities was $317,582 thousand against $217,533 thousand a year earlier, and the first-half net loss was $355,393 thousand.

**Base case.** There is no defensible base case and supplying one would be dishonest. Joby has no meaningful revenue, negative EBITDA, and no cash-flow history the model can use - normalized FCFF is not merely negative, it is unavailable, computed over zero years. The value of this company is a probability-weighted terminal outcome conditional on FAA type certification, and neither a discounted cash flow nor a trailing multiple can express that. The correct output is no_model.

**Devil's advocate.**
- Strongest counter: That refusing to model is an abdication, and the multiples table does contain information: a blended midpoint of $3.77 against a $6.13 price says Joby is 38.5% OVERVALUED, and declining to say so is a failure of nerve dressed up as rigour. The p_tbv row in particular is not obviously meaningless - Joby has real tangible assets in aircraft, tooling and a Marina production facility, and at 3.7x tangible book against a cohort median of 3.5x it is being valued roughly in line with other capital-intensive industrials while producing none of their revenue.
- What would prove it: An FAA type certification decision or a further formal slip in the certification timeline - the single event that determines whether the tangible book is worth a premium or a discount.
- Already visible today: The counter is right that I should not smuggle in a bullish conclusion, and I want to be explicit that refusing to model is NOT a judgement that Joby is cheap - at 84.3x EV/sales against a cohort median of 1.6x it may well be enormously expensive, and I cannot say so with a defensible number. But the counter's own evidence defeats it. The two rows do not corroborate each other, they contradict each other: ev_sales implies $1.63 to $1.75 while p_tbv implies $3.32 to $11.68, so one row says the equity is worth a quarter of the price and the other says it could be worth nearly double. Per the RIOT/EPRT rule, rows that straddle the price in opposite directions mean the blend is meaningless, and the brief itself states the cohort is NOT RANKED - too few comparable Industrials names to define a distribution honestly. Averaging an 84x EV/sales multiple on a company whose revenue has not arrived against a tangible book that is mostly pre-revenue tooling produces a number with a decimal point and no content. The $3.77 blended midpoint should be disregarded entirely.
- Left unresolved: The certification timeline itself, which is the only variable that matters and which neither the company nor the FAA has committed to a date for. I resolved the funding question rather than leaving it open (see data_quality_note), but no amount of balance-sheet work substitutes for a certification date, and I have none.

**Key risks.** FAA type certification is binary, unscheduled and outside the company's control; Operating cash burn ACCELERATING: $317.6M in H1 2026 against $217.5M a year earlier, roughly $635M annualised before capex; Dilution from the Blade acquisition earn-out: 2.42 million shares in the September 2026 resale prospectus, plus the item 3.02 issuance of 2026-08-11; Sector-wide de-rating with Archer and EHang at 52-week lows indicates a narrowing funding window for all eVTOL issuers; $6.1B market capitalisation on essentially no revenue leaves the entire value in a terminal assumption
**Watch for.** FAA type certification decision or a further formal timeline slip; Whether the operating burn keeps accelerating from the $317.6M half-year rate; Whether Texas eIPP and Dubai operations produce disclosed commercial revenue; Any further item 3.02 unregistered equity sales despite the current liquidity

**Data quality.** All four flags are correct and jointly decisive: nonpositive_normalized_fcff, negative_ebitda_valued_on_revenue_or_gross_profit_only, negative_fcf_year_in_window and speculative_cost_of_debt. The FCFF base is computed over ZERO years, so there is no reverse DCF at all and no implied growth to compare anything against. The multiples fallback must also be refused, on three independent grounds from the RIOT/EPRT and MXL rules: the two rows straddle the price in OPPOSITE directions (ev_sales $1.63-$1.75 versus p_tbv $3.32-$11.68, a factor of two to seven apart); the cohort is explicitly NOT RANKED for want of comparable Industrials names; and EV/sales of 84.3x against a cohort median of 1.6x is the pre-revenue case CLAUDE.md already warns carries almost no information, because the denominator describes revenue that has not arrived. I record explicitly that refusing is not a judgement that JOBY is cheap - it may well be expensive - and that the blended $3.77 midpoint and its -38.5% gap should be disregarded rather than read as a bearish signal. I did go and get the observable the refusal depends on, rather than recording it as unavailable: the 2026-08-06 10-Q shows $2,263,826 thousand of cash and short-term investments at 2026-06-30 against first-half operating cash burn of $317,582 thousand, which is roughly three and a half years of runway at the current rate before capex. That materially WEAKENS the obvious bear framing - Joby is not in a near-term dilution spiral, and the August item 3.02 issuance is why liquidity rose from $1.41B to $2.26B rather than evidence of distress. It does not change the verdict, because a funded pre-revenue manufacturer is still unmodellable by a discounted cash flow, but it changes the risk profile and it was one grep away in a document already fetched.
