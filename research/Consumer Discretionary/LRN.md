**The case for the price.** Stride's core General Education business has turned, and the exit quarter says so plainly: FY2026 Q4 revenue was $636.1M against $653.6M (-2.7%) and Q4 adjusted operating income $117.8M against $130.6M (-9.8%), against a full year that still printed +4.7% revenue and +6.9% adjusted operating income. General Education revenue fell 2.1% on enrollments -2.5%; gross margin fell 140bp to 37.8% on platform investment; and management says FY2027 applications are tracking slightly BEHIND last year with revenue per enrollment flat to up slightly. The growth engine is Career Learning (+19.1%), which is now carrying a shrinking majority business. A stock that fell 48% in 252 days is pricing the inflection from a compounder to a low-growth business with a competitive overhang from AI-native learning tools - which Stride's own risk factors name explicitly ('entry of new competitors with superior technologies (including artificial intelligence)').

**What changed.** FY2026 10-K filed 2026-08-05 and the 2026-08-04 results release: full-year revenue $2,518.1M vs $2,405.3M, adjusted operating income $498.4M vs $466.2M. FY2027 commentary: revenue per enrollment flat to up slightly, applications tracking slightly behind, and the company does not expect to cut off enrollments as early as it did in FY2026. No formal FY2027 numeric guidance in the release.
