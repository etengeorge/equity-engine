**The case for the price.** At $28.23 Dana is not a standalone company being valued, it is half of a pending combination. On 2026-06-11 Dana agreed to combine with Eaton's Mobility Group in a Reverse Morris Trust: Eaton first separates Mobility to its own shareholders by split-off or spin-off, then Dana merges with a Mobility subsidiary and SURVIVES AS A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY of the Mobility Group, with EATON shareholders owning at least 50.1% and Dana shareholders about 49.9% of the combined company. The price is therefore an opinion about a $5.1 billion transaction - roughly 8.3x estimated 2026 pro forma adjusted EBITDA before synergies, 5.9x including $250 million of run-rate synergies - producing a company with about $11 billion of sales and $1.7 billion of adjusted EBITDA on a fully synergised pro forma basis, targeting $14-15 billion of sales and 18% margins by 2030. The stock is up 44.5% over twelve months on that, plus a 2026 sales guidance raise to $7.65-7.85 billion and resumed buybacks.

**What changed.** Two structural transactions inside twelve months, neither of which the engine knows about. First, Dana divested its Off-Highway business: revenue_series steps from $10,284 million to $7,500 million between the two newest years, equity_now is $1,920 million against an equity_series[0] of $840 million (2.29x), and total debt is down to $1,357 million - the gain and the deleveraging land in the first half of 2026, after the fiscal year the cash-flow history describes. Second, the pending Eaton Mobility combination announced 2026-06-11, closing expected in the first quarter of 2027 and reported on track as of September 2026, under which Eaton receives a cash distribution of about $1.1 billion funded through NEW DEBT raised prior to closing. Byron Foster became CEO on 2026-07-01. Operationally, Q2 2026 sales were $2,010 million against $1,935 million with a net LOSS of $5 million, full-year sales guidance was raised to $7.65-7.85 billion while adjusted EPS guidance was lowered, and $250 million of Eaton synergies are targeted within 24 months of closing.
