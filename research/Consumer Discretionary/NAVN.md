**The case for the price.** Navan is a 2025-vintage IPO growing gross booking volume 45% to over $3 billion a quarter and revenue 35% to $233 million, with full-year guidance raised to roughly 32% growth at the midpoint. On the true share count of 260.4 million the market capitalisation is about $7.06 billion, so the market is paying roughly 9x enterprise value to trailing revenue for a corporate travel and expense platform. The case for that price is category replacement: an AI-native alternative to Concur in a market where the incumbent is widely disliked, with payments and expense attached to the booking flow so the revenue per customer compounds. The case against is in the same quarter's numbers - the stock fell more than 15% on the Q2 print despite beating, because operating costs and losses widened on AI spend and the newly acquired BoomPop events platform. The company lost $398.0 million on $702.3 million of revenue in fiscal 2026 with $182.1 million of stock compensation, and the thing a DCF would discount does not yet exist.

**What changed.** Q2 fiscal 2027 (reported 2026-09-09): revenue $233M, up 35%; GBV up 45% to over $3 billion; full-year guidance raised to approximately 32% growth at the midpoint; free cash flow described on the call as having turned positive; the stock fell over 15% on rising AI-related costs and the BoomPop acquisition. The BoomPop acquisition of an AI-powered meetings and events platform was announced around 2026-09-13, after the 2026-07-31 balance sheet.
