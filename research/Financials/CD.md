## 2026-09-25 — NO_MODEL (conviction: medium)

- **Verdict:** no_model · price $5.17

**The case for the price.** There is no defensible case for this price on any cash-flow or book measure, and saying so is the honest output. Chaince Digital Holdings - formerly Mercurity Fintech Holding - carries a market capitalisation of $568.7M on annual revenue of $1,867,068 and tangible common equity of about $46.6M. That is roughly 300x revenue and 12x tangible book, for a company that lost money in each of the last three years. What the market is buying is a narrative - tokenisation, on-chain innovation and a FINRA-registered broker-dealer subsidiary that acts as exclusive sales agent on at-the-market programmes for other micro-cap Nasdaq issuers - and a float small enough that $2M of news can move it 78%. The steelman is that the regulated brokerage licence plus a tokenisation platform is an option on a large market; the option has a strike nobody has priced and the company has $1.9M of revenue to show for it.

**What changed.** The 8-K that won this name an opportunistic slot does not explain the move. On 2026-09-24 Chaince announced it had repurchased and permanently cancelled ALL warrants issued in its November 2023 private placement for aggregate cash consideration of US$2.0 million, closing 2026-09-21, with no shares issued and no warrants now outstanding. That is a $2.0M anti-dilutive housekeeping transaction in a $568.7M market capitalisation - mildly positive, and not remotely proportionate to a +75.3% five-day and +50.3% twenty-one-day move. Third-party pricing shows the shares at $3.50 on 2026-09-21 against a $2.91 prior close and a $5.17 close on 2026-09-24, so the move is real and it happened across 22-24 September. I searched for another catalyst and found none. Other recent activity: an approximately $16.2M registered direct offering announced 2026-08-10 'to advance its digital asset and capital markets strategy', and Chaince Securities engagements as exclusive ATM sales agent for Top Wealth Group ($200M), Wetour Robotics ($50M) and ZJK Industrial ($9.8M).
