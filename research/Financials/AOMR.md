## 2026-10-02 — NO_EDGE (conviction: low)

- **Verdict:** no_edge · price $7.27

**The case for the price.** Angel Oak Mortgage REIT trades at 0.72x GAAP book and 0.59x economic book because the market has watched this manager destroy roughly half the book value since the June 2021 IPO: $19.00 a share then, $10.13 of GAAP book now, dividends notwithstanding. It is externally managed by Falcons I, LLC, so a base fee accrues on equity whatever the return, and the incentive fee is excluded from the company's own Distributable Earnings measure. It runs about 10.6x leverage ($2,493M of debt against $235M of equity) in non-QM mortgage credit, funded on three-month rolling warehouse facilities - the 2026-09-25 8-K extends one of them to 2026-12-28 and discloses the rolling-renewal structure in terms. For a vehicle with that funding profile, that leverage and that record, a persistent discount to book is not a mispricing, it is the market declining to capitalise a return it does not expect to see again.

**What changed.** Q2 2026 (filed 2026-08-04): GAAP book value $10.13 per share and economic book value $12.24, down 1.7% and 0.3% from Q1. Distributable Earnings $9.0M or $0.37 per diluted share, with Distributable Earnings Return on Average Equity of 14.6% annualised for the quarter and 10.8% for the half, against 4.2% and 5.5% in the prior-year periods. Net interest income $10.7M, +8% y/y. Dividend declared $0.32 per share, paid 2026-08-28 - covered by distributable earnings at an 86% payout. On 2026-09-25 (8-K filed 2026-09-29, item 1.01) the company extended a loan financing facility with 'Multinational Bank 1' from 2026-09-25 to 2026-12-28 and cut the interest rate pricing spread from 1.30-2.10% to 1.20-2.10% - a mildly favourable routine renewal, and the 'filed an 8-K in the last few sessions' signal that won this name an opportunistic slot. Nothing in it is a catalyst.

- Already visible today: The deciding half is visible and it goes the wrong way for the bull: GAAP book fell 1.7% in the quarter and economic book 0.3%, and GAAP book per share is down from about $11.52 at 2025-12-31 to $10.13 at 2026-06-30 - roughly 12% in six months - while the engine reports a 16.5% GAAP return for the newest year. One year of improvement after a year of 5.5% does not distinguish them, and the five-year record - $19.00 of IPO book to $10.13 - argues for the cycle.
