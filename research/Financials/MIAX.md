**Base case.** No sustainable ROTCE can honestly be supplied, because price to tangible book is the wrong model for this business and supplying a return would launder that category error into a fair value. MIAX is a technology-driven exchange operator: its productive assets are matching-engine software, SEC exchange licences, member relationships and order flow, which are either expensed as incurred or carried as intangibles that the tangible-book construction then strips out before dividing. The brief's own numbers say so — actual price to tangible book of 4.81 against tangible book of $6.53 a share, where the standing lesson sets roughly 4x as the signature of a company whose capital is not its balance sheet. A 54% adjusted EBITDA margin on $131M of quarterly revenue is not a return on $6.53 of tangible equity in any meaningful sense. The model compounds that with a numerator defect: the 'sustainable ROTCE' of -10.8% is a three-year GAAP average carrying `loss_year_in_window`, and MIAX only listed recently, so the window spans the pre-profitability years of an exchange build-out. The company earning $0.47 of GAAP diluted EPS this quarter is not the company that average describes. That negative return then produces a justified multiple of -1.67, i.e. the model asserts the equity is worth less than nothing, against a business with 54% margins and 20% revenue growth.
