**The case for the price.** Travere is being repriced as FILSPARI (sparsentan) converts from an accelerated-approval story into a real rare-disease franchise. Q2 2026 total revenue was $169.6M against $114.4M a year earlier, up 48%, and the first half was $296.8M against $196.2M, up 51%. The GAAP net loss of $34.8M masks a non-GAAP adjusted net loss of only $9.0M for the quarter and $4.8M for the half — the company is essentially at breakeven and about to cross it, on a product with orphan economics in IgA nephropathy and an FSGS opportunity behind it. That is why the stock is up 208.7% over 252 days and 42.5% over 63. A market cap of $6.2B on a franchise annualising toward $700M of revenue with high gross margin, a nephrology salesforce already built, and operating leverage from here is a growth valuation, not a cash-flow one — and the market is paying for the next three years of the ramp, not for trailing cash flow.
