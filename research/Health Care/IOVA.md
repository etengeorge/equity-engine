**The case for the price.** The market pays $3.58bn for a commercial-stage cell-therapy company with a launched product, no debt and no profits. Amtagvi (lifileucel) passed $100M of US revenue in a single quarter for the first time; total Q3 revenue was about $122M, up roughly 80% year on year; gross margin reached a record ~60% from 56% in Q2; full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $410-420M was reaffirmed; and management says current cash funds operations well into 2029. On about $415M of 2026 revenue the equity is on roughly 8.6x sales for an autologous TIL platform with label-expansion readouts in lung cancer and sarcoma. The price is a bet on the platform rather than on melanoma, and that is why the stock is up roughly 325% over twelve months and down 40% over the last twenty-one days: on 2026-11-05 the company reported a 24.6% objective response rate in 69 patients with non-squamous metastatic NSCLC — durable, but modest on an unselected population — against 59.1% in the biomarker-selected subgroup. The market marked down the breadth of the lung opportunity while keeping the selected-population case.

**What changed.** The lung readout arrived and it is the reason for the 40% drawdown. The 2026-11-05 release reports a 24.6% ORR in 69 patients with non-squamous mNSCLC from cohorts 1 and 2 of IOV-LUN-202, with 59.1% in the biomarker-selected subgroup, and 55.6% confirmed ORR in nine patients with undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma and dedifferentiated liposarcoma, for which lifileucel won Fast Track designation. Commercially the quarter was strong: revenue about $122M, up ~80%, US Amtagvi up ~77%, gross margin to ~60%, 2026 guidance of $410-420M reaffirmed, cash runway stated into 2029, and new POSITILITY and OPTILISM trials added on the strength of the biomarker-selected data. None of it changes the valuation question: the company remains cash-flow negative in every year of the window, with operating cash flow of -$302.4M, -$353.0M and -$361.8M.
