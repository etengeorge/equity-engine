**The case for the price.** At $16.51 Dyne is a clinical-stage company with no product revenue, $676.1M of cash at 2026-06-30 plus the proceeds of an upsized July offering, a cash runway management states reaches the second quarter of 2028, and operating cash outflow of $403.2M in the last year and widening. A $3.08B market capitalisation is a probability-weighted claim on three things: a Biologics License Application for z-rostudirsen in exon 51 Duchenne muscular dystrophy that the FDA accepted with PRIORITY REVIEW and a PDUFA target action date of 2027-01-21; DYNE-101 (z-basivarsen) in myotonic dystrophy type 1, where enrolment of 71 participants in the registrational expansion is complete; and the FORCE platform behind a third programme, DYNE-302 in FSHD, cleared for IND in July. That is a binary, dated set of bets, and the 36.5% fall over twenty-one days is the market repricing one of them.

**What changed.** The twenty-one-day decline has a specific, dated cause and it is not Dyne's own data. On 2026-09-08 Novartis announced that del-desiran MISSED the primary endpoint in the pivotal Phase 3 HARBOR study in myotonic dystrophy type 1; Novartis fell as much as 10% and Dyne fell 31% premarket in sympathy, with InvestorsHub noting no Dyne-specific catalyst. RBC and others argued the read-across is limited because the two programmes differ in how they target the transferrin receptor, in payload and in trial design. Dyne's own newsflow since: the BLA for z-rostudirsen in exon 51 DMD accepted with Priority Review, PDUFA 2027-01-21, and registrational expansion enrolment of 71 participants complete (2026-07-29); FDA clearance of the DYNE-302 IND in FSHD (2026-07-28); closing of an upsized public offering with full exercise of the underwriters' option (2026-07-23); additional one-year ACHIEVE data for z-basivarsen in DM1 to be presented at World Muscle Society and AANEM in late September 2026 against a matched natural-history cohort; preclinical DMD candidates for exons 53, 45, 44 and 55 (2026-09-03); inducement grants in August and September.

- What would prove it: Whether price-to-tangible-book means anything for a pre-revenue biotech. For a clinical-stage company tangible book is essentially the cash pile, so P/TBV is a restatement of price-to-cash - and the comparison is only informative if the cohort's book is also cash, which for 328 mixed Health Care names it is not. The 2027-01-21 PDUFA decision is the observable that actually settles the value.
- Already visible today: Yes, and it defeats the counter. The multiples row fails the standing refusal test on two counts: the cohort is reported as NOT RANKED - too few comparable Health Care names to define a distribution honestly - and there is only ONE row, p_tbv, so there is no cross-row corroboration and nothing to disagree with itself. A single unranked row on a pre-revenue company is the RIOT/EPRT case, and the rule is to disregard it rather than average a nonsense input into a verdict. The denominator is also stale: the balance sheet is 2026-06-30 while the upsized offering closed 2026-07-23, so both cash and book are understated.
- Left unresolved: The clinical questions, entirely. I cannot assess how far the transferrin-receptor targeting, payload and trial-design differences RBC cites actually insulate DYNE-101 from the del-desiran failure - that is a biology judgement, and the one-year ACHIEVE data presented at WMS and AANEM in late September 2026 is the relevant evidence, which I could not obtain because it postdates the filings and no report of it is in the news store. I also did not obtain the size of the July offering, so I cannot state pro-forma cash beyond management's own runway statement of Q2 2028.
