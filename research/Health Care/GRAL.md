**The case for the price.** At $158.92 and a $7.1bn market capitalisation, GRAIL is not being valued on cash flow — it is a priced option on a pending FDA decision and the Medicare reimbursement that would follow. On 2026-09-23 the Molecular and Clinical Genetics Devices Panel of the FDA's Medical Devices Advisory Committee voted in favour of approving the Galleri multi-cancer early detection test for adults aged 50 and over, after the agency's own briefing documents raised no major safety or accuracy concerns and after PATHFINDER 2 data published in Nature Medicine. The stock rose 33% on the briefing documents alone and 55% in that week, and is up 131.8% over 63 days and 25.1% over the last five. The premarket approval application was submitted in January 2026 and the decision is still outstanding. The reason approval matters is reimbursement rather than permission: Galleri is already sold in the United States as a laboratory-developed test without FDA approval, and Congress has already passed legislation enabling Medicare to reimburse multi-cancer early-detection tests once a test wins agency authorisation, with coverage reported as possible from 2028. Commercially the test is working — second-quarter revenue of $44M, up 26%, on volume up 35% to more than 61,000 tests. So the consensus case is coherent: a near-term regulatory event unlocks a payer channel for a product with demonstrated demand in a very large screening population.

**What changed.** The FDA advisory committee voted favourably on 2026-09-23 — the single most important event in this company's history and well after the fiscal 2025 10-K the model's history rests on. Trading was halted on the day of the panel. The stock is up 131.8% over 63 days. For the most recent five days, during which it rose a further 25.1%, I could NOT identify a company-specific catalyst and I want that recorded rather than filled in: the brief's news store holds 22 items but nothing dated after 2026-09-25, GRAIL has filed nothing with the SEC since the 2026-08-06 10-Q, and two web searches returned no GRAIL-specific news for the week of 2026-10-26. The most likely explanation is anticipation of the pending premarket approval decision plus sell-side repositioning, but I cannot evidence it, so my confidence that 'nothing happened' is low. What I did establish is that as of today there is NO final FDA approval — the panel vote is non-binding, though the agency usually follows it — and that the decision is expected within months. On the financials, the 10-Q for the half year shows net cash used in operating activities of $167.7M against $172.0M a year earlier, so the burn is running near $335M a year, and the second-quarter net loss was $110M.
