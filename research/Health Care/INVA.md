# INVA — INNOVIVA
*Health Care · Russell 2000*

Append-only research log. Newest entries at the bottom.

## 2026-09-25 — NO_EDGE (conviction: low)

- **Verdict:** no_edge · price $20.66
- **Not repriced:** not a repriceable fcff name or no growth supplied

**The case for the price.** At $20.66 Innoviva holds $570.4M of cash and $669.5M of strategic healthcare investments - $1,240M, or $17.16 per share of a $20.66 price - so the market is paying roughly $3.50 a share, about $253M of equity value, for the operating business. It does that because the operating business is mostly a royalty that dies. Gross GSK royalty revenue is $59.8M a quarter, ~$239M a year, and the principal patent covering RELVAR/BREO ELLIPTA expires 2030-10-11 with pediatric exclusivity to 2031 - four to five years out, i.e. INSIDE the explicit DCF window. The market is also discounting the investment portfolio heavily, and rightly: $457.7M of the $669.5M is Armata Pharmaceuticals, a clinical-stage bacteriophage company whose mark just cost Innoviva $161.0M in one quarter and which Innoviva keeps funding through successive term loans. That is not cash, it is a commitment.

**What changed.** Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-05): revenue $119.6M against $100.3M, income from operations $50.9M against $48.8M, but a net LOSS of $83.4M ($1.14 basic) driven by $161.0M of unfavourable fair-value change on equity and long-term investments, principally Armata's share price. Gross GSK royalty revenue $59.8M, described by the company as 'remains stable', +2% sequentially. Innoviva Specialty Therapeutics net product sales $51.8M, +46% year over year ($36.6M US, $15.2M ex-US: GIAPREZA $21.0M, XACDURO $12.0M, XERAVA $3.3M), with the company on track for at least $150M of US net product sales in 2026. Royalty and product receivables $110.6M, +25% y/y. Nortiva Bio launched 2026-06-18 as a clinical-stage subsidiary. XACDURO, ZEVTERA and NUZOLVENCE were named 2026 Prix Galien USA finalists.

**Base case.** No single five-year FCFF growth rate describes this company, and supplying one would launder a structural error into a fair value. Innoviva is three things a single two-stage FCFF model cannot add up: a royalty stream with a contractual and dated end, a specialty pharma growing net product sales 46% with patents running to 2033-2045, and a $669.5M portfolio of marked investments dominated by one illiquid controlled biotech. The engine's terminal value capitalises whatever the fifth year produces at 2% growth IN PERPETUITY, and roughly 58% of today's revenue contractually stops around 2030-31. That is not a growth-rate problem, it is the wrong model form, and the schema has no field to override terminal growth.

**Devil's advocate.**
- Strongest counter: My refusal leans on the cliff, and the cliff argument is weaker than it looks. Generic Advair has existed since 2016 and RELVAR/BREO royalties are still 'stable' a decade later, because the ELLIPTA device and formulation are hard to copy - inhaled generics routinely fail to arrive on schedule. If the royalty runs materially past 2031, then a market requiring a 23-38% ANNUAL decline in FCFF is far too pessimistic and the name is straightforwardly cheap: the enterprise value corrected for the investment portfolio is roughly $511M against $187.5M of normalised FCFF, which is 2.7x.
- What would prove it: Two things. Any ANDA filing or Paragraph IV notice against FF/VI or UMEC/VI, and GSK's own disclosure of Ellipta franchise erosion - GSK reports Trelegy/Breo/Anoro sales quarterly, and a step-down in Innoviva's royalty line would appear within a quarter of it. And the trajectory of IST: whether $150M of 2026 US sales becomes $300M+ by 2029, which is what replacing the royalty requires.
- Already visible today: Not yet, and that is the honest state of it. The royalty is stable TODAY at $59.8M a quarter and +2% sequentially, which supports the counter. I found no ANDA or Paragraph IV disclosure against FF/VI in the 10-K or 10-Q, and the 10-K discusses generic competition only as a risk factor. Against the counter, the 10-K says in terms that royalties from RELVAR/BREO and ANORO 'are expected to represent the majority of our foreseeable future revenues from GSK', so the concentration is not disputed.
- Left unresolved: The date. I could not find the end of the LABA Collaboration Agreement royalty term in either the 10-K or the 10-Q - the agreement runs to the last-to-expire patent rather than to a stated date, and the filings do not tabulate those patents for the GSK-partnered products the way they do for the owned products. The 2030-10-11 expiry plus pediatric exclusivity to 2031 is from a secondary patent database, not from a filing, and I am recording it as such. Also unresolved: HealthCare Royalty is entitled to quarterly royalties on worldwide GIAPREZA net sales until 2031 or a cap, which is a real claim on the IST cash flows that appears in neither total_debt nor the enterprise value, and I could not size its remaining balance.

**Key risks.** ~58% of revenue is a GSK royalty on two inhaled products whose principal patent protection lapses around 2030-31, inside the valuation window; $457.7M of the $669.5M investment portfolio is Armata, an illiquid controlled clinical-stage position that cost $161.0M of marks in one quarter and which Innoviva keeps lending to; The HealthCare Royalty obligation on GIAPREZA sales to 2031 is a claim on IST cash flow that sits in neither debt nor EV; Net income is dominated by marks, so reported earnings say almost nothing about the operating business in any given quarter
**Watch for.** Any Paragraph IV / ANDA notice on fluticasone furoate-vilanterol or umeclidinium-vilanterol; GSK's quarterly Ellipta franchise disclosure and any step-down in Innoviva's royalty line from the $59.8M quarterly run rate; IST US net product sales against the $150M 2026 commitment, and whether the 46% growth rate holds into 2027; Further Armata funding rounds or term loans - each one converts a marked asset into a cash commitment

**Data quality.** No flags were raised and two inputs are still wrong, in OPPOSITE directions, which is why no fair value is recorded. (1) Enterprise value omits the investment portfolio. valuation.enterprise_value deducts cash plus short_term_investments only; Innoviva's $669.5M of strategic healthcare investments ($457.7M Armata, $177.3M other strategic equity and convertible debt, $34.5M ISP Fund) are LONG-TERM and are not deducted. Correcting it takes EV from $1,180M to $511M and the growth the market requires from -23.5% to -38.3%; deducting only the non-Armata $211.8M gives -27.1%. This error makes the name look LESS cheap than the model's own logic implies. (2) Against that, the model's fixed 2% PERPETUAL terminal growth is applied to a company where the majority revenue line has a dated contractual end around 2030-31 - the IRWD/HRMY dated-cliff signature, here on a pure royalty rather than a drug, and it makes the name look FAR cheaper than it is. That is the whole +226.8% gap and the 97th percentile of 91 Health Care names. (3) The naive baseline of +4.1% is itself unusable: revenue_series runs [411.3, 358.7, 310.5, 331.3, 391.9, 336.8] newest first - a dip and recovery, not a trend, and it mixes a declining royalty with a 46%-growth product business. (4) beta 0.32 with R-squared 0.052 gives a 6.60% WACC, the lowest in today's ten; on the standing low-R-squared rule that beta is measurement error and the direction is a false buy. (5) total_debt of $258.5M at a 6.46% implied rate sits inside the normal band so neither the >12% nor the <2% detector can fire, but Innoviva's debt is convertible senior notes and the TDOC lesson says the extractor's coverage of ConvertibleNotesPayable concepts is incomplete - I could not verify the $258.5M against the debt note. Two large errors in opposite directions is the SM rule: no_edge, no number.
