**The case for the price.** At $65.57 Celcuity is a $3.2 billion option on a drug that went on sale three weeks before this screen priced the stock. REVTORPYK (gedatolisib) was approved by the FDA on 2026-07-14 and became commercially available in the United States on 2026-09-30 - the first and only approved therapy inhibiting all four class I PI3K isoforms plus mTORC1 and mTORC2, for HR+/HER2- advanced breast cancer WITHOUT a PIK3CA mutation after progression on endocrine therapy. An sNDA for the PIK3CA-MUTANT population, a second and larger slice of the same indication, was submitted on 2026-08-26. A buyer is paying for a launch curve in a large indication plus a label expansion, funded by about $754 million of cash and investments, with 80 oncology specialists in the field and NCCN guideline inclusion. That is a probability-weighted revenue forecast, and it is a perfectly rational thing for a market to price. It is not a discounted cash flow.

**What changed.** REVTORPYK became commercially available on 2026-09-30 (item 7.01/8.01 8-K with EX-99.1), three weeks before the price date, so there is no revenue history of any kind: revenue_series in the extract is EMPTY. The sNDA for the PIK3CA-mutant population was submitted 2026-08-26. David Gryska - former CFO of Incyte and of Celgene - joined the board on 2026-10-02 as the company transitions to commercial stage. In June 2026 Celcuity refinanced: it priced $500 million of convertible notes on 2026-06-03 and TERMINATED the prior loan agreement, under which $130 million had been drawn ($100 million at 2024-05-30 plus $30 million on 2025-09-09). The tape is the striking part: the stock is DOWN 18.0% over 21 days and 20.6% over 63 days, and flat over twelve months, i.e. the market has de-rated the name THROUGH its own approval and launch.
