**The case for the price.** At $106.20 the market is paying ~$5.9B of market capitalisation — roughly $4.7B of enterprise value against $1.2B of cash — for claseprubart, an anti-C1s antibody being developed as a 'pipeline-in-a-product' across generalised myasthenia gravis, CIDP and multifocal motor neuropathy, plus DNTH212. The bull case is specific and dated: the Phase 3 CAPTIVATE Interim Responder Analysis showed a 75% response rate in the first 40 participants completing Part A, against a 50% target set by precedent for aC1s inhibition, with no related serious infections, no drug-induced lupus, no related SAEs and no safety discontinuations. The complement class in gMG and CIDP is commercially proven, and a subcutaneous Q4W dosing option is a real differentiator against incumbents. The stock is +180.2% over twelve months on that.

**What changed.** Q2 2026 (2026-08-04): Phase 3 EMERGE in gMG initiated June 2026, top-line 2H'28. CAPTIVATE Part B top-line guidance expected by year-end 2026. Phase 2 MoMeNtum in MMN fully enrolled, top-line December 2026. Phase 1 healthy-volunteer data for DNTH212 by year end. R&D expense $48.7M in the quarter against $26.3M a year earlier. Net loss $50.2M ($0.90/share). Approximately $1.2B of cash, cash equivalents and investments at 2026-06-30, guided to fund operations into 2030.
