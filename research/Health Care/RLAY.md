**Data quality.** A CORRECT REFUSAL, AND A NEW AND MEASURABLE EXTRACTION DEFECT ON THE WAY TO IT. The engine refused on `nonpositive_normalized_fcff` with `negative_fcf_year_in_window`, which is right for a clinical-stage company. But ENTERPRISE VALUE IS OVERSTATED BY 27% AND THE CAUSE IS STRUCTURAL, NOT PER-NAME. The extract holds `cash = $124,184,000` and `short_term_investments = 0.0`; the 2026-09-30 balance sheet reads 'Cash and cash equivalents $124,184' and, on the next line, 'Investments $724,458', and the company's own release states 'approximately $849 million in cash, cash equivalents and investments'. So $724.5M — 85% of the liquidity and 21% of market capitalisation — is invisible to enterprise value, which the engine computes as $3,391M against a true $2,667M. The cause is the alias list: `FIELDS['short_term_investments']` reads only `ShortTermInvestments`, `AvailableForSaleSecuritiesDebtSecuritiesCurrent` and `MarketableSecuritiesCurrent`, so it reaches only the CURRENT tranche under two concept names, while this filer's XBRL carries contexts including `InvestmentsWithMaturityOfOneToTwoYearsMember` — a biotech funding a multi-year runway in one-to-two-year paper has the whole treasury outside those three aliases. `short_term_investments` is zero or null on 1,291 of 1,956 names in this screen; most of those genuinely hold no securities, but EWTX in today's same ten is the second verified instance ($1,675.2M invisible, enterprise value overstated 61%), so this is a population and not an anecdote. DIRECTION: an undeducted investment balance OVERSTATES enterprise value, overstates implied growth and manufactures RICHNESS — the INVA direction, a false sell — which is why it has gone unnoticed, and it lands hardest on exactly the cohort where net cash is most of the value. Other inputs are sound: the share count of 220,959,470 comes from the dei cover-page concept dated 2026-10-30 and matches the 10-Q cover exactly; `total_debt` of zero is correct and `interest_expense` of null is therefore harmless rather than the CON defect. One negative result worth recording so the next pass does not 'fix' it: `revenue_series` of [$15.4M, $10.0M, $25.5M, $1.4M, $3.0M, $82.7M] trips the HRI, TNET and MARA step-change detector and is NOT an extraction defect — it is the real lumpiness of collaboration and milestone income, the AMLX precedent.
