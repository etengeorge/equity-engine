It sells through U.S. department and specialty stores, its own DTC stores/websites, and international partners, and sources most product from Asia.

**Charts (rendered in the dashboard):** Revenue rose while operating margin collapsed to a 3.2% trough (FY25)

**Relationships (second-order graph):** China (sourcing/tariffs) (input); Section 122 / IEEPA tariff regime (regulator); Kurt Geiger (partner); Vietnam / Cambodia / Mexico / Brazil (supplier); U.S. department & specialty retailers (customer)

**Our differentiated view:** The market is capitalizing a transitory margin trough as if it were structural. Operating margin should partially recover from 3.2% as tariff exposure is removed and Kurt Geiger integrates, so base-case FCFF growth runs modestly above the implied ~10.2%.
