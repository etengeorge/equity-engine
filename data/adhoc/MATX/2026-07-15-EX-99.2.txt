Preliminary 2Q26 Results
• Matson had a strong 2Q26 with momentum in our China service carrying over from the
post-Lunar New Year period
• Our CLX and MAX services saw higher-than-expected freight rates and demand across
e-commerce, garments and e-goods against a backdrop of tighter supply conditions in
the Transpacific tradelane
• Looking ahead, we expect our China service to be at or near capacity through peak
season
─ Supported by current Transpacific market conditions and our expectation of
continued solid U.S. consumer demand
• In our domestic ocean tradelanes, we saw lower YoY volumes in Hawaii and Alaska and
higher YoY volume in Guam
• In Logistics, operating income increased YoY primarily due to higher contributions from
freight forwarding and transportation brokerage, partially offset by a lower contribution
from warehousing
• Expects 2Q26 consolidated operating income to be $153.0 to $160.0 million
