 Burford Reports 2Q26 and YTD26 Financial Results 

 Burford Capital Limited (“Burford”), the leading global finance and asset management firm focused on law, today announces its unaudited financial results for the three and six months ended June 30, 2026 (“2Q26” and “YTD26”, respectively). The full detailed presentation of Burford’s 2Q26 and YTD26 financial results, as well as detailed portfolio data table, can be viewed at http://investors.burfordcapital.com. 

 Christopher Bogart, Burford Capital’s Chief Executive Officer, commented : 

 “Burford generated more cash this quarter than any since the first quarter of 2025, and July saw strong momentum in the portfolio. 

 “With $733 million of cash on hand, Burford is well-positioned to continue growing its industry-leading platform while strong liquidity and robust cash receipts allow us to prudently manage the balance sheet.”

 About Burford Capital 

 Burford Capital is the leading global finance and asset management firm focused on law. Its businesses include litigation finance and risk management, asset recovery and a wide range of legal finance and advisory activities. Burford is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: BUR) and the London Stock Exchange (LSE: BUR) and works with companies and law firms around the world from its global network of offices.
