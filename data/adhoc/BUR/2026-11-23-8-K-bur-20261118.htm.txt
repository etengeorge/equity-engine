 Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. 

 On November 18, 2026, the board of directors (the “ Board of Directors ”) of Burford Capital Limited (the “ Company ”), upon the recommendation of the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee of the Board of Directors, appointed James W. Kilman Jr. (“ Mr. Kilman ”) as a non-executive director of the Company, effective January 1, 2027. Mr. Kilman will stand for annual election as a non-executive director at the Company’s annual general meetings, commencing in 2027. 

 Mr. Kilman (65) has extensive experience in investment banking and financial services, having worked with the management and boards of directors of diversified financial services companies to provide advice on strategy, mergers and restructurings as well as on debt and equity capital raising. Prior to his retirement in 2016, Mr. Kilman served as Vice Chairman of Investment Banking at Morgan Stanley. During his 32-year investment banking career, he also held senior positions at Goldman Sachs, ABN AMRO and PaineWebber. Since 2016, Mr. Kilman has served as Chief Executive Officer of KielStrand Capital LLC, a family office. He currently serves as trustee and Chair of the Pricing Committee of the series of trusts comprising MFS Mutual Funds, Boston, MA, a leading US mutual fund complex, a role he has held since 2019. Mr. Kilman has a Master of Arts and a Bachelor of Arts in Economics from Yale University. 

 Mr. Kilman previously served as the Company’s Chief Financial Officer from 2019 to 2021 and was employed as a Senior Advisor from 2017 to 2019 and from 2021 until June 30, 2026. As a result of his prior employment by the Company, Mr. Kilman will not be considered an independent director under the listing standards of the New York Stock Exchange and the applicable rules and regulations of the US Securities and Exchange Commission (the “ SEC ”) upon his appointment to the Board of Directors. Mr. Kilman will become eligible for an independence determination on July 1, 2029 and is not expected to serve on any committees of the Board of Directors until that time. 

 There are no arrangements or understandings between Mr. Kilman and any other persons pursuant to which he was appointed as a director, and there are no family relationships between Mr. Kilman and any of the Company’s other directors or executive officers. 

 Mr. Kilman will receive the same compensation for his service and the same indemnification as the Company’s other non-executive directors, as described in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed with the SEC on April 2, 2026. 

 In connection with his prior employment with the Company, Mr. Kilman received aggregate compensation of approximately $400,000 in 2025 and $325,000 in 2026, consisting of salary and equity-based long-term incentive compensation. In addition, Mr. Kilman participated in the Company’s employee benefit plans. Following the termination of his employment, Mr. Kilman continues to hold restricted share units awarded under the Company’s long-term incentive compensation plans (all of which are vested as a result of the application of the Company’s retirement policy) and participate in the Company’s deferred compensation plan, in each case, pursuant to arrangements established during his employment. In addition, Mr. Kilman (through KielStrand Capital LLC) has a commitment of $500,000 to Burford Opportunity Fund B LP and received $61,082 in distributions in 2025 and no distributions in 2026 through the date of this Current Report on Form 8-K. Except as described above, there are no transactions involving Mr. Kilman that are required to be disclosed pursuant to Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K.
