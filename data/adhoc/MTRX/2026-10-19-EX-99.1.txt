 MATRIX SERVICE COMPANY APPOINTS DOMINIC A. SAVARINO AS SENIOR VICE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER 

 HOUSTON, TX – October 19, 2026 – Matrix Service Company (Nasdaq: MTRX), a leading heavy industrial contractor that engineers, constructs, and maintains critical energy, power, and industrial infrastructure, today announced the appointment of Dominic A. Savarino as Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, effective November 6, 2026. This appointment further strengthens Matrix’s leadership team as the Company executes its growth strategy and focuses on delivering long-term shareholder value. Mr. Savarino will be based in Houston and report directly to Shawn P. Payne, President and Chief Executive Officer. 

 As Chief Financial Officer, Mr. Savarino will lead Matrix’s finance and accounting organization, including investor relations, treasury, tax, FP&A, and financial reporting. 

 Mr. Savarino previously served as Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of Diamond Offshore Drilling Inc., a NYSE-listed multinational offshore drilling firm, where he played a key role in strengthening the company’s capital structure, engaging with investors and analysts, and supporting its merger with Noble Corporation. His expertise in capital markets, treasury, restructuring, and investor relations contributed directly to significant EBITDA growth, margin expansion, and enhanced shareholder value. 

 Previously, Mr. Savarino held several executive leadership roles at McDermott International, Inc., a provider of engineering and construction solutions for the energy industry, including as Vice President and General Manager – Americas, Vice President, Finance – Asia Pacific, and Vice President, Tax. In addition to his finance leadership experience, Mr. Savarino held direct P&L responsibility for a major operating business, providing a valuable operational and commercial perspective. In that capacity, he oversaw multidisciplinary engineering and construction teams, played a pivotal role in delivering large-scale, complex energy-industry projects, and implemented best practices in safety, quality, and operational excellence. 

 Mr. Savarino holds both a Bachelor of Business Administration and a Master of Professional Accounting from the University of Texas and is a Certified Public Accountant in Texas. 

 “We are pleased to welcome Dominic to Matrix Service Company,” said Shawn P. Payne, President and Chief Executive Officer of Matrix Service Company. “His record of financial leadership in complex, growth-oriented environments and his industry expertise will be instrumental as we execute our strategy to deliver superior returns and long-term value for our shareholders.” 

 “I am excited to join Matrix at such a dynamic moment for the company and our industry,” said Dominic A. Savarino. “Matrix’s strong reputation for delivery of quality projects, combined with its strategic vision for growth, makes this an exceptional opportunity. I look forward to working with Shawn and the Matrix team to help build on the company’s momentum and deliver value for all stakeholders.” 

 About Matrix Service Company. Matrix Service Company (Nasdaq: MTRX) is a leading heavy industrial contractor that engineers, constructs, and maintains critical energy, power, and industrial infrastructure. The Company’s commitment to safety, quality, and integrity has earned it a leadership position in providing infrastructure solutions across multiple end markets. Matrix’s work is foundational to helping energy, power, and industrial clients achieve their objectives, positively impact quality of life through the products they provide and 

 improve the efficiency and resilience of their critical infrastructure. Matrix prides itself on its culture, its commitment to its core values, and on being consistently certified as a Great Place To Work®. 

 The Company maintains its principal executive offices in Houston, Texas with offices located throughout the United States and Canada, as well as Sydney, Australia, and Seoul, South Korea. The Company reports its financial results in three key operating segments: Storage and Terminal Solutions, Utility and Power Infrastructure, and Process and Industrial Facilities.
