 Item 1.01 

Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

On October 16, 2026, Medallion Financial Corp. (the “ Company ”)

 entered into a Purchase and Settlement Agreement (the “ Purchase Agreement ”) with BIMIZCI Fund LLC (“ BIMIZCI ”) and ZimCal Asset Management LLC (“ ZimCal ” and, together with BIMIZCI, the “ Investor Parties ”).

Pursuant to the Purchase Agreement, the Company has agreed to purchase from BIMIZCI trust preferred securities of Medallion Financing Trust I having
 a liquidation amount of $15,000,000 (the “ Trust Preferred Securities ”) for an aggregate cash purchase price equal to $11,049,000, plus accrued but unpaid
 distributions on the Trust Preferred Securities. The closing of the repurchase is subject to customary closing conditions.

The Purchase Agreement further provides, among other things, that:

 • 

The Investor Parties shall be subject to customary standstill restrictions until October 16, 2031 (the “ Standstill
 Period ”), including, among other things, restrictions on initiating proxy contests or conducting proxy solicitations, acquiring any securities, voting rights or indebtedness of the Company and its subsidiaries, or making or
 assisting in transaction proposals, subject to certain exceptions.

 • 

During the Standstill Period, the Company and the Investor Parties shall not threaten, institute, solicit, join, or knowingly encourage or assist a lawsuit against the other party, subject to
 certain exceptions.

 • 

Until October 16, 2028, the Company and the Investor Parties shall not make public or certain private statements that disparage, defame, slander, or constitute a knowingly false statement of
 material fact regarding the other party or its representatives, subject to certain exceptions.

 • 

The Investor Parties have also agreed to sell, transfer, and otherwise dispose of all securities of the Company, including shares of the Company’s common stock, that are beneficially owned by the
 Investor Parties or any of their respective affiliates, associates, and investor principals by no later than December 31, 2026.

 • 

The Purchase Agreement provides that if any party initiates a legal proceeding arising out of or relating to the Purchase Agreement, the prevailing party or person is entitled to recover reasonable
 attorneys’ fees and costs incurred in connection with such legal proceeding from the non-prevailing party or person, with the prevailing party or person being determined as set forth in the Purchase Agreement.

 • 

In addition to the liability of the Investor Parties for breaches of the Purchase Agreement, the Purchase Agreement further provides that a related person of the Investor Parties may be subject to
 monetary damages or equitable relief in connection with certain potential breaches of certain provisions of the Purchase Agreement, including the standstill, non-disparagement, and litigation covenants, by certain related persons of the
 Investor Parties, subject to limitations set forth in the Purchase Agreement.

 • 

The Purchase Agreement will remain in effect until the end of the Standstill Period. In addition, certain obligations of each party will terminate upon an uncured material breach of certain
 provisions of the Purchase Agreement by the other party. If the closing of the acquisition of the Trust Preferred Securities has not occurred on or before the twentieth business day after the date of the Purchase Agreement, the Purchase
 Agreement may be terminated by either party, subject to certain exceptions.

The foregoing summary of the Purchase Agreement does not purport to be complete and is subject to, and qualified in its entirety by,
 the full text of the Purchase Agreement, which is attached as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
