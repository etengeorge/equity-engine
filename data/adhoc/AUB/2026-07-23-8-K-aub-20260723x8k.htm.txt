 Item 8.01 Other Events. 

 ​ 

 On July 23, 2026, Atlantic Union Bankshares Corporation (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing that its Board of Directors (the “Board”) declared a quarterly dividend of $0.37 per share of common stock. The dividend is payable on August 21, 2026 to common shareholders of record as of August 7, 2026. 

 ​ 

 The Company also announced that the Board declared a quarterly dividend on the Company’s 6.875% Perpetual Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock, Series A (the “Series A preferred stock”). The Series A preferred stock is represented by depositary shares, each representing a 1/400 th ownership interest in a share of Series A preferred stock. The dividend of $171.88 per share (equivalent to $0.43 per outstanding depositary share) is payable on September 1, 2026 to holders of record as of August 17, 2026. 

 ​ 

 A copy of the Company’s press release dated July 23, 2026 is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference .
