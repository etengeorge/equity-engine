 RPC, Inc. Appoints Rob Saltiel as President and Chief Executive Officer 

 ​ 

 Three-time public company CEO brings more than 30 years in energy and oilfield services, 
 with a proven record of operational rigor and disciplined capital allocation 

 ​ 

 Executive Chairman Richard Hubbell to retire at year-end; 
 Patrick Gunning appointed Non-Executive Chairman of the Board 

 ​ 

 ATLANTA, November 18, 2026 — RPC, Inc. (NYSE: RES), a leading diversified oilfield services company, today announced the appointment of Rob Saltiel as President and Chief Executive Officer, and as a member of the Board of Directors effective November 20, 2026. Mr. Saltiel will succeed Ben M. Palmer, whose planned retirement was announced in June 2026, after 30 years with RPC. 

 ​ 

Mr. Saltiel brings more than three decades of leadership experience across the energy and oilfield services sectors, including serving as CEO of multiple publicly traded companies. Most recently, Mr. Saltiel served as President and CEO of MRC Global from 2021 through its merger with DNOW in 2025. Under Mr. Saltiel’s leadership at MRC, the company significantly improved its margin profile while diversifying its business toward less cyclical and higher-growth end markets. Prior to his time at MRC, Rob served as President and CEO of Key Energy Services and Atwood Oceanics, and held senior leadership roles at Transocean, among other organizations. Mr. Saltiel holds a B.S. in chemical engineering from Princeton University and earned his MBA from Northwestern University.

 ​ 

In addition, the Company has announced that Executive Chairman Richard A. Hubbell intends to retire at year-end 2026, after four decades with RPC, beginning in 1987 as President and later as CEO from 2003 until 2022. Patrick Gunning, a seasoned audit and finance leader who joined RPC’s Board in 2021 and currently serves as its Lead Independent Director, will succeed him as Non-Executive Chairman, effective January 1, 2027. 

 ​ 

“The RPC Board set out to identify a leader with a proven operating record in oilfield services, an ability to create value through market cycles, and a developed vision on the industry’s direction. Rob is an ideal fit for each of those criteria, having spent his career leading with an operator’s mentality, improving margins and returns, and investing for growth,” said Mr. Gunning. “With thanks to what Ben and the team have built, RPC has the portfolio and financial flexibility to pursue meaningful growth as the market improves, and we believe Rob is the right leader to guide the company forward.”

 ​ 

Mr. Saltiel commented, "I am honored and thrilled to accept the role as RPC’s next CEO. RPC is an outstanding company with nearly 3,000 talented team members, leading-edge completions technologies, a strong culture of customer service, and a solid balance sheet. I look forward to leveraging the strong foundation established under Rick and Ben’s leadership, and helping RPC grow for the benefit of our shareholders, customers, and team members."

 ​ 

Mr. Gunning added, “Rick and Ben have been integral as builders and stewards of RPC, helping the business maintain discipline through every kind of market, preserving strategic and financial flexibility, and mentoring many along the way. The entire Board and management team are deeply grateful to them both for their leadership, contributions, and the example they have set for everyone at RPC.”

 ​ 

Mr. Hubbell concluded, “Leading RPC has been the work of my career, and I am proud of what our people have built together. With Rob as CEO and Pat stepping into the Chairman role, RPC has the right

 About RPC, Inc. 

RPC provides a broad range of specialized oilfield services and equipment primarily to independent and major oilfield companies engaged in the exploration, production and development of oil and gas properties throughout the United States, including the Gulf of America, mid-continent, southwest, Appalachian and Rocky Mountain regions, and in selected international markets. RPC’s investor website can be found on the internet at RPC.net.
