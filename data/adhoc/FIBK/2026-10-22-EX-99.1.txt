 For Immediate Release 

 First Interstate BancSystem, Inc. Reports Third Quarter Earnings 

 Billings, MT - October 22, 2026 - First Interstate BancSystem, Inc. (NASDAQ: FIBK) (the “Company”) today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026. For the quarter, the Company reported net income of $62.2 million, or $0.65 per diluted share, which compares to net income of $83.9 million, or $0.87 per diluted share, for the second quarter of 2026 and net income of $71.4 million, or $0.69 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. The second quarter of 2026 includes a $19.5 million gain related to the sale of the eleven Nebraska branches. 

 HIGHLIGHTS 

 • Net interest margin increased to 3.53% for the third quarter of 2026, an 8-basis point increase from the second quarter of 2026 and a 19-basis point increase from the third quarter of 2025. 

 • Non-performing assets decreased $10.0 million, or 6.1%, to $155.0 million as of September 30, 2026, from $165.0 million as of June 30, 2026 and decreased $30.6 million, or 16.5%, from $185.6 million as of September 30, 2025. 

 • Criticized loans increased slightly by $2.1 million to $939.5 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $937.4 million as of June 30, 2026, and decreased $224.6 million, compared to $1,164.1 million as of September 30, 2025. 

 • Net charge-offs increased $0.2 million to $9.9 million, or an annualized 0.28% of average loans outstanding, as of September 30, 2026, from $9.7 million, or an annualized 0.27% of average loans outstanding, as of June 30, 2026, and increased $7.6 million from $2.3 million, or an annualized 0.06% of average loans outstanding, as of September 30, 2025. 

 • Total deposits decreased $52.8 million, or 0.2%, to $21,388.5 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $21,441.3 million as of June 30, 2026, across all interest bearing deposit categories during the third quarter of 2026, which were partially offset by an increase in noninterest bearing deposits. Total deposits decreased $1,216.5 million, or 5.4%, compared to $22,605.0 million as of September 30, 2025, across all deposit categories, primarily driven by the sale of the Arizona and Kansas branches during the fourth quarter of 2025, which included $641.6 million of deposits, as well as the sale of eleven Nebraska branches during the second quarter of 2026, which included $244.2 million of deposits. 

 • During the third quarter of 2026, the Company repurchased approximately 2.04 million shares of common stock for a total repurchase of $76.3 million. The Company has repurchased approximately 10.02 million shares of its common stock through September 30, 2026 for a total repurchase of $346.6 million since the board of directors authorized the repurchase program in August of 2025. As of September 30, 2026, approximately $103.4 million remained available for future purchases under the $450.0 million authorized since its adoption in August of 2025. 

 • Common equity tier 1 capital ratio decreased 18 basis points during the third quarter of 2026 to 14.36%, compared to the second quarter of 2026, primarily due to shares repurchased, partially offset by lower risk-weighted assets driven by lower loan balances during the third quarter of 2026. 

 • Appointed Jeff Lee as the Company’s Executive Vice President and Chief Operations Officer, effective September 14, 2026. 

 “We are pleased to see meaningful improvement in our net interest margin and an increase in non-interest bearing deposits, as we remain focused on strengthening the quality and earnings power of the franchise,” said James A. Reuter, President and Chief Executive Officer of the Company. “We also continued to execute on our previously announced share repurchase authorization, underscoring our commitment to shareholder returns. Through disciplined capital deployment, operating model optimization, and continued investment in relationship growth, we are building a more efficient organization and remain confident in our ability to deliver improving returns over time.” 

 DIVIDEND DECLARATION 

 On October 21, 2026, the Company’s board of directors declared a dividend of $0.47 per common share, payable on November 13, 2026, to common stockholders of record as of November 3, 2026. The dividend equates to a 5.0% annualized yield based on the $37.82 per share average closing price of the Company’s common stock as reported on NASDAQ during the third quarter of 2026.

 NET INTEREST INCOME 

 Net interest income increased $4.0 million to $206.2 million during the third quarter of 2026, compared to net interest income of $202.2 million during the second quarter of 2026. Net interest income decreased $0.6 million, or 0.3%, during the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025. The quarterly increase from the second quarter of 2026 was primarily driven by higher yields on interest earning assets and lower interest expense on time deposits, partially offset by lower interest income due to reduced loan balances and interest earning assets. Year-over-year lower interest earning assets and interest bearing liabilities were partially influenced by the reduction in loans and deposits related to the sale of the Arizona and Kansas branches during the fourth quarter of 2025 and the sale of eleven Nebraska branches during the second quarter of 2026, which resulted in a reduction of net interest income in the third quarter of 2026. 

 Interest accretion attributable to the fair value of acquired loans, related to prior acquisitions, contributed to net interest income during the third quarter of 2026, the second quarter of 2026, and the third quarter of 2025, in the amounts of $2.4 million, $3.5 million, and $3.5 million, respectively. 

 Net interest margin ratio was 3.53% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 3.45% during the second quarter of 2026, and 3.34% during the third quarter of 2025. Net FTE (fully-taxable equivalent) interest margin ratio 1 was 3.55% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 3.48% during the second quarter of 2026, and 3.36% during the third quarter of 2025. Excluding interest accretion from the fair value of acquired loans, the adjusted net FTE interest margin ratio 1 was 3.51%, an increase of 9 basis points from the prior quarter, primarily driven by higher yields on interest earning assets and lower rates on interest bearing time deposits, partially offset by lower average loan balances and higher security balances. Excluding interest accretion from the fair value of acquired loans, on a year-over-year basis, the adjusted net FTE interest margin ratio increased 21 basis points, primarily as a result of lower cost of deposits and an improved funding mix, partially offset by lower average loan balances. 

 PROVISION FOR CREDIT LOSSES 

 During the third quarter of 2026, the Company recorded a provision for credit losses of $10.5 million. This compares to a reduction of provision for credit losses of $3.2 million during the second quarter of 2026 and no provision during the third quarter of 2025. 

 For the third quarter of 2026, net loan charge-offs were $9.9 million, or an annualized 0.28% of average loans outstanding, compared to net loan charge-offs of $9.7 million, or an annualized 0.27% of average loans outstanding, for the second quarter of 2026 and net loan charge-offs of $2.3 million, or an annualized 0.06% of average loans outstanding, for the third quarter of 2025. Net loan charge-offs in the third quarter of 2026 were composed of charge-offs of $11.9 million offset by recoveries of $2.0 million. Net loan charge-offs in the second quarter of 2026 were composed of charge-offs of $14.0 million, which were offset by recoveries of $4.3 million. Net loan charge-offs in the third quarter of 2025 were composed of charge-offs of $6.7 million, which were offset by recoveries of $4.4 million. 

 The Company’s allowance for credit losses as a percentage of period-end loans held for investment was 1.30% at September 30, 2026, compared to 1.28% at June 30, 2026 and 1.30% at September 30, 2025. Coverage of non-performing loans increased to 120.1% at September 30, 2026, compared to 114.1% at June 30, 2026 and 113.0% at September 30, 2025.

 NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES 

 In addition to results presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America, or GAAP, this press release contains the following non-GAAP financial measures that management uses to evaluate our performance relative to our capital adequacy standards: (i) tangible common stockholders’ equity; (ii) tangible assets; (iii) tangible book value per common share; (iv) tangible common stockholders’ equity to tangible assets; (v) average tangible common stockholders’ equity; (vi) return on average tangible common stockholders’ equity; (vii) net FTE interest income; (viii) net FTE interest margin ratio; (ix) adjusted net FTE interest income; and (x) adjusted net FTE interest margin ratio. Tangible common stockholders’ equity is calculated as total common stockholders’ equity less goodwill and other intangible assets (excluding mortgage servicing rights). Tangible assets are calculated as total assets less goodwill and other intangible assets (excluding mortgage servicing rights). Tangible book value per common share is calculated as tangible common stockholders’ equity divided by common shares outstanding. Tangible common stockholders’ equity to tangible assets is calculated as tangible common stockholders’ equity divided by tangible assets. Average tangible common stockholders’ equity is calculated as average total stockholders’ equity less average goodwill and other intangible assets (excluding mortgage servicing rights). Return on average tangible common stockholders’ equity is calculated as annualized net income available to common shareholders divided by average tangible common stockholders’ equity. Net FTE interest income is calculated as net interest income, adjusted to include its FTE interest income. Net FTE interest margin ratio is calculated as net FTE interest income divided by average interest earning assets. Adjusted net FTE interest income is calculated as net FTE interest income less purchase accounting interest accretion on acquired loans. Adjusted net FTE interest margin ratio is calculated as annualized adjusted net FTE interest income divided by average interest earning assets. These non-GAAP financial measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies because other companies may not calculate these non-GAAP measures in the same manner. They also should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. 

 The Company adjusts the most directly comparable capital adequacy GAAP financial measures to the non-GAAP financial measures described in subclauses (i) through (vi) above to exclude goodwill and other intangible assets (except mortgage servicing rights), adjusts its GAAP net interest income to include fully taxable equivalent adjustments and further adjusts its net interest income on a fully taxable equivalent basis to exclude purchase accounting interest accretion. Management believes these non-GAAP financial measures, which are intended to complement the capital ratios defined by banking regulators and to present on a consistent basis our and our acquired companies’ organic continuing operations without regard to acquisition costs and other adjustments that we consider to be unpredictable and dependent on a significant number of factors that are outside our control, are useful to investors in evaluating the Company’s performance because, as a general matter, they either do not represent an actual cash expense and are inconsistent in amount and frequency depending upon the timing and size of our acquisitions (including the size, complexity and/or volume of past acquisitions, which may drive the magnitude of acquisition related costs, but may not be indicative of the size, complexity and/or volume of future acquisitions or related costs), or they cannot be anticipated or estimated in a particular period (in particular as it relates to unexpected recovery amounts). This impacts the ratios that are important to analysts and allows investors to compare certain aspects of the Company’s capitalization to other companies.
