 Hub Group Receives Nasdaq Approval for Continued Listing and Extension to Complete Delayed Filings 

OAK BROOK, Ill., Nov. 17, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Hub Group, Inc. (Nasdaq: HUBG) (“Hub Group” or the “Company”) today announced
that, on November 11, 2026, it received a decision letter (the “Decision Letter”) from the Nasdaq Hearings Panel (the “Hearings Panel”) approving the continued listing of the Company’s Class A common stock on
the Nasdaq Global Select Market (“Nasdaq”), subject to the Company demonstrating compliance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(c)(1) (the “Filing Rule”) on or before December 31, 2026. This decision followed a hearing on
October 27, 2026, at which the Company presented its plan to regain compliance with the Filing Rule and requested a continued stay of the delisting of its Class A common stock from the Nasdaq Global Select Market pending its return to
compliance. 
 
To regain compliance with the Filing Rule, the Company will be required to file its Form 10-K for the
year ended December 31, 2025 and its Form 10-Qs for the periods ended March 31, 2026, June 30, 2026 and September 30, 2026 (the “Delinquent Filings”). The Company’s
finance and accounting team continues to work expeditiously to complete the restatement process and file the Delinquent Filings. However, the Company cannot confirm that it will be able to complete all of the Delinquent Filings before
December 31, 2026. If each of the Delinquent Filings is not made by December 31, 2026, the Company anticipates requesting that the Hearings Panel extend the exception period. The Hearings Panel informed the Company that it does not have
the discretion to extend the exception period to a date later than March 12, 2027. 
 
The Company also announced that, on November 17, 2026, it
received an expected notice from Nasdaq indicating that the Company is not in compliance with the Filing Rule as a result of its failure to timely file its Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30,
2026. The notice has no immediate effect on the Hearings Panel’s Decision Letter or the continued listing of the Company’s Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. 

Additional information is included in the Company’s Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the SEC on
November 17, 2026.
