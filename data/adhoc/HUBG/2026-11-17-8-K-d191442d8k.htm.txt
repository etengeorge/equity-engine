 Item 3.01. 
 
Notice of Delisting or Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard; Transfer of Listing. 

On November 17, 2026, Hub Group, Inc. (the “Company”) received an expected notice (the “Notice”) from the listing qualifications staff of The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (“Nasdaq”) notifying the Company that, as a result of the Company not having timely filed its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 (the “Q326 Form 10-Q”), it is not in compliance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(c)(1) (the “Filing Rule”), which requires companies with securities listed on Nasdaq to timely file all required periodic reports with the Securities and Exchange Commission. 
 
The Notice provides that the Company has until November 24, 2026 to submit a plan to regain compliance with the Filing Rule, including its plan to file the Q326 Form 10-Q. The Company intends to timely submit to Nasdaq confirmation that it is working to regain compliance with the Filing Rule in a manner consistent with the Decision Letter (as defined below). 
 
The Notice has no immediate effect on the listing or trading of shares of the Company’s Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. 

 Item 7.01. 
 
Regulation FD Disclosure. 

On November 11, 2026, the Company received a decision letter (the “Decision Letter”) from the Nasdaq Hearings Panel (the “Hearings Panel”) approving the continued listing of the Company’s Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market, subject to the Company demonstrating compliance with the Filing Rule on or before December 31, 2026 and certain other conditions. 
 
As previously disclosed, on September 16, 2026, the Company received a Staff Delisting Determination (the “Staff Determination”) from the listing qualifications staff of Nasdaq notifying the Company that Nasdaq had initiated a process to delist the Company’s Class A common stock as a result of the Company not being in compliance with the Filing Rule. The Staff Determination was issued because the Company had not filed its Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025 and its Quarterly Reports on Form 10-Q for the periods ended March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026 (such filings, together with the Q326 Form 10-Q, the “Delinquent Filings”). The Company timely appealed the Staff Determination by requesting a hearing before the Hearings Panel and requested, and was granted, a stay of the suspension of trading of its Class A common stock pending the outcome of the hearing. 
 
On October 27, 2026, the Company attended a hearing before the Hearings Panel at which it presented its plan to regain compliance with the Filing Rule and requested a continued stay of the suspension and delisting of its Class A common stock from the Nasdaq Global Select Market pending its return to compliance. 
 
The Company’s finance and accounting team continues to work expeditiously to complete the restatement process and file the Delinquent Filings. However, the Company cannot confirm that it will be able to complete all of the Delinquent Filings on or before December 31, 2026. If each of the Delinquent Filings is not made by December 31, 2026, the Company anticipates requesting that the Hearings Panel extend the exception period. The Hearings Panel informed the Company that it does not have the discretion to extend the exception period to a date later than March 12, 2027. 
 
The Decision Letter requires the Company to provide prompt notification of any significant events occurring during the exception period that may affect the Company’s compliance with Nasdaq requirements, including any event that may call into question the Company’s ability to meet the terms of the exception granted. The Hearings Panel has reserved the right to reconsider the terms of the exception based on any event, condition or circumstance that exists or develops that would, in the opinion of the Hearings Panel, make continued listing of the Company’s securities on Nasdaq inadvisable or unwarranted. The Decision Letter also provides that the Nasdaq Listing and Hearing Review Council may, on its own motion, determine to modify, reverse, dismiss or remand the decision of the Hearings Panel. The foregoing summarizes certain terms of the Hearings Panel’s decision and does not purport to describe all of the terms and conditions of the Decision Letter. 
 
There can be no assurance that the Company will be able to satisfy the conditions of the Decision Letter, including filing the Delinquent Filings within the exception period granted by the Decision Letter. If the Company does not satisfy these conditions and is not granted relief from such conditions, the Hearings Panel may determine to suspend trading in and delist the Company’s Class A common stock. 

On November 17, 2026, the Company issued a press release announcing its receipt of the Notice and the Decision Letter. The press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
