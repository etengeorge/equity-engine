Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. 
 
 Equity and Asset Purchase Agreement 
 
On November 2, 2026, CVR Energy, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “ Company ”), entered into an Equity and Asset Purchase Agreement (the “ Purchase Agreement ”) with Marathon Petroleum Company LP, Tesoro Refining & Marketing Company LLC, Tesoro Logistics Operations LLC, Western Refining Southwest LLC, Western Refining Terminals, LLC and Marathon Petroleum Supply and Trading LLC (collectively, the “ Sellers ”), each a subsidiary or affiliate of Marathon Petroleum Corporation. 
 
Pursuant to the Purchase Agreement, the Company has agreed to acquire (i) 100% of the outstanding membership interests of St. Paul Park Refining Co. LLC, which owns the St. Paul Park Refinery, (ii) 17% of the outstanding common interests of Minnesota Pipe Line Company, LLC, (iii) 17% of the outstanding common stock of MPL Investments, Inc. and (iv) certain other assets of the Sellers, including the Mandan Refinery, the related marketing business and terminal, pipeline, rack, tankage and other logistics assets (collectively, the “ Target Assets ”) and to assume certain liabilities related to the Target Assets, other than specified liabilities to be retained by the Sellers. The acquisition of the Target Assets is referred to herein as the “ Acquisition .” 
 
 Summary of the Terms of the Purchase Agreement 
 
 Consideration. The Company has agreed to purchase the Target Assets for an aggregate base cash purchase price of $4,200 million, subject to certain customary closing adjustments, including net working capital adjustments, in each case as of the closing of the Acquisition (the “ Acquisition Closing ”), and subject to other specified adjustments. The Company will also assume certain liabilities relating to the Target Assets, including environmental liabilities, whether arising before, on or after the Acquisition Closing, other than certain liabilities retained by the Sellers. 
 
 Representations, Warranties, Covenants and Indemnification. The Purchase Agreement contains customary representations, warranties and covenants of the Company and the Sellers. The representations, warranties and pre-closing covenants of the Sellers generally do not survive the Acquisition Closing, and the Company will not have post-closing indemnification rights in respect of breaches thereof, subject to specified exceptions, including for Fraud (as defined in the Purchase Agreement). Concurrent with and effective upon execution of the Purchase Agreement, the Company has arranged a representation and warranty insurance policy intended to provide coverage in the event of any breaches of certain representations and warranties, subject to applicable policy limits, exclusions, deductibles and other terms and conditions. Following the Acquisition Closing, the Sellers and the Company have agreed to indemnify each other for certain liabilities and certain breaches of the Purchase Agreement, subject to certain limitations as more particularly set forth in the Purchase Agreement. The Purchase Agreement’s covenants include obligations of the Sellers concerning the operation of the Target Assets in the ordinary course during the period between the date of the Purchase Agreement and the Acquisition Closing, subject to specified exceptions, and obligations of the parties to use reasonable best efforts to obtain required antitrust approvals. The Company is not required to accept a specified Burdensome Condition (as defined in the Purchase Agreement), including a requirement to divest either acquired refinery or the Company’s Coffeyville or Wynnewood refinery. The Company is also required to use reasonable best efforts to arrange and obtain the financing for the Acquisition (including alternative financing in specified circumstances), and the Sellers are required to use reasonable best efforts to provide customary cooperation in connection with such financing, subject to specified limitations. The Company has also represented that it has, and at all times through the Acquisition Closing will have, at least $500 million of unrestricted cash and cash equivalents, which amount will increase, upon notice to the Sellers, by up to an additional $500 million upon the deposit of the proceeds of an equity offering completed by the Company after the date of the Purchase Agreement. 
 
 Closing Conditions. Completion of the Acquisition is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions, including expiration or termination of applicable waiting periods under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended (the “ HSR Act ”), and other applicable antitrust laws; the absence of legal prohibitions and specified proceedings challenging the Acquisition or seeking substantial damages in connection with it; and the accuracy of the parties’ representations and warranties and material compliance with their covenants, subject to applicable qualifications. The Company’s obligation to close is also subject to the absence of a Material Adverse Effect (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) that has occurred since the date of the Purchase Agreement and is 

continuing at the Acquisition Closing, and receipt of written approval of, or other written non-objection to or authorization for, the transfer of the Mandan refinery’s hazardous waste permit by the North Dakota Department of Environmental Quality. The Acquisition is not subject to a financing condition. 
 
 Casualty and Condemnation. In the event of specified casualty or condemnation losses affecting the Target Assets prior to the Acquisition Closing, the Purchase Agreement provides, in specified circumstances and at the Sellers’ election, for the repair or restoration of the affected assets (which may delay the Acquisition Closing), a purchase price reduction or the assignment to the Company of related insurance or condemnation proceeds, but does not provide either party a right to terminate the Purchase Agreement. 
 
 Termination. The Purchase Agreement contains termination rights for the Company and the Sellers, including if the Acquisition Closing has not occurred within 270 days after execution of the Purchase Agreement, subject to extension to as late as 360 days after execution in specified circumstances relating to antitrust approvals or casualty restoration. Upon termination in specified circumstances, the Company may be required to pay a reverse termination fee equal to (i) 2% of the aggregate base purchase price, or $84 million, for failure of the antitrust closing conditions by the applicable outside date, including following the Company’s permitted rejection of a Burdensome Condition; (ii) 6%, or $252 million, for the Company’s Willful Breach (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) or a qualifying failure to close because required financing is not obtained or funded; or (iii) 4%, or $168 million, for other specified failures to close. The reverse termination fees are not cumulative. The MPC Sellers (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) may be required to pay the Company a termination fee equal to 5% of the aggregate base purchase price for the Sellers’ Willful Breach or 4% for other specified failures to close. The Purchase Agreement also provides for specific performance in certain circumstances, subject to the conditions and limitations set forth therein. 
 
The foregoing description of the Purchase Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Purchase Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 2.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K (this “ Current Report ”) and incorporated herein by reference.

 Commercial Investor Equity Investment 
 
On November 2, 2026, in connection with the execution of the Purchase Agreement, the Company entered into a subscription agreement (the “ Subscription Agreement ”) with Trafigura US Holdings, Inc. (the “ Commercial Investor ”), pursuant to which the Commercial Investor agreed to purchase approximately 14,989,695 shares of the Company’s Series A-1 non-voting preferred stock (the “ Commercial Preferred Stock” and, together with any additional shares of preferred or common stock issued as delay compensation, the “ Commercial Investor Shares ”) at a base price of $53.37 per share (the “ Investment Price ”), for an aggregate purchase price of $800,000,000 (the “ Commercial Investor Equity Investment ”). The Commercial Preferred Stock will be convertible at the holder’s election into shares of the Company’s common stock, $0.01 par value per share (the “ Common Stock ”), initially on a one-for-one basis upon expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the HSR Act. The Commercial Investor may not convert the Commercial Preferred Stock if such conversion would result in the Commercial Investor’s beneficial ownership of Common Stock exceeding 9.50% of the total outstanding Common Stock, which limitation may be waived at the 

election of the Commercial Investor upon no less than sixty-one (61) days’ notice. The Commercial Preferred Stock ranks pari passu with the Common Stock, has no right to receive dividends or distributions not payable to holders of Common Stock (other than for certain specified delays), and is otherwise substantially equivalent to the Common Stock, but does not have general voting rights. The Subscription Agreement also provides for certain pricing adjustments, including for certain lower priced equity financings between the date of the Subscription Agreement and the earlier of the Acquisition Closing and termination of the Purchase Agreement in accordance with its terms, and the issuance of additional shares of Commercial Preferred Stock (or, at closing, Common Stock at the Commercial Investor’s election) as compensation for specified delays in consummation of the Acquisition or receipt of HSR Act approval for conversion of the Commercial Preferred Stock, subject to applicable conditions. Such credits will begin to accrue six months after signing, each at 1% per annum, and may accrue concurrently. The Company intends to use the proceeds of the Commercial Investor Equity Investment to partially finance the aggregate consideration for the Acquisition. In connection with the Acquisition and the Commercial Investor Equity Investment, the Company and certain of its subsidiaries entered into commercial agreements and related arrangements with affiliates of the Commercial Investor, as described below under “Commercial Agreements.” 
 
The Commercial Investor’s obligation to consummate the Commercial Investor Equity Investment is subject to the concurrent satisfaction or waiver of the conditions to the closing of the Acquisition under the Purchase Agreement (other than conditions to be satisfied at closing, subject to their satisfaction or waiver at closing), the substantially concurrent consummation of the Acquisition, material compliance with the agreed capital plan, receipt of the specified financing and certain other customary closing conditions. The Subscription Agreement will terminate in specified circumstances, including upon termination of the Purchase Agreement in accordance with its terms. The Commercial Investor Shares will be issued in a private placement exempt from registration under Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “ Securities Act ”). 
 
In connection with the Commercial Investor Equity Investment, concurrently with execution of the Subscription Agreement, the Company has entered into an Investor Rights Agreement (the “ Investor Rights Agreement ”) with the Commercial Investor, pursuant to which the Commercial Investor is entitled upon closing to certain demand, piggyback and shelf registration rights with respect to the Common Stock. Pursuant to the Investor Rights Agreement, the Commercial Investor is entitled to designate a board observer to the board of directors of the Company, which the Commercial Investor may convert at its election into the right to designate one nominee for election to the board of directors of the Company, for so long as the Commercial Investor retains at least 50% of its equity position acquired upon consummation of the Acquisition. Pursuant to the Investor Rights Agreement, the Commercial Investor is also entitled to certain information and preemptive rights upon consummation of the Acquisition, subject to certain conditions. The preemptive right applies from signing to qualifying issuances at or below the Commercial Investor’s subscription price. 
 
The foregoing description does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Subscription Agreement, including the Investor Rights Agreement attached thereto, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report and incorporated herein by reference. 
 
 Commercial Agreements 
 
On November 2, 2026, in connection with the execution of the Purchase Agreement and the Commercial Investor Equity Investment, a subsidiary of the Company entered into a crude oil supply agreement (the “ Supply Agreement ”) with Trafigura Trading LLC (the “ Commercial Counterparty ”) with respect to the Target Assets. The Company’s subsidiaries and the Commercial Counterparty also entered into other commercial agreements relating to the Target Assets (collectively with the Supply Agreement, the “ Commercial Agreements ”). The Commercial Agreements will become effective upon the later of the Acquisition Closing and the satisfaction or waiver of applicable conditions, including funding of the Commercial Investor Equity Investment, and will expire on the tenth anniversary of that effective date, subject to an extension option exercisable by the Commercial Counterparty. 
 
Under the Commercial Agreements, the Commercial Counterparty will purchase the initial crude oil and feedstock inventories at the Mandan and St. Paul Park refineries and, under the Supply Agreement, will serve as their exclusive crude oil supply and intermediation counterparty, subject to specified exceptions. Under the Supply Agreement, the Company’s subsidiaries will determine the crude slate and nominate monthly volumes, the Commercial Counterparty will source crude oil from third parties consistent with those nominations, and title will pass to the Company’s subsidiaries as crude oil is withdrawn from designated refinery storage tanks. The Company’s subsidiaries will continue to operate the refineries 

and retain exposure to refining margins. The Company’s subsidiaries will also provide related storage, logistics and transportation services and temporarily assign specified transportation and storage rights to the Commercial Investor. 
 
The foregoing descriptions of the Commercial Agreements do not purport to be complete. The description of the Supply Agreement is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Supply Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 10.2 to this Current Report and incorporated herein by reference. 
 
 IEP Subscription Agreement 
 
On November 2, 2026, the Company entered into a subscription agreement (the “ IEP Subscription Agreement ”) with American Entertainment Properties Corp. (“ AEP ”), a wholly-owned corporate subsidiary of Icahn Enterprises Holdings L.P., pursuant to which AEP committed, subject to the terms and conditions thereof, to purchase shares of the Company’s Series A-2 non-voting mandatory convertible preferred stock (the “ IEP Preferred Stock ”) for an aggregate purchase price of up to $500,000,000 at a price per share equal to the Investment Price (the “ IEP Commitment ”). The IEP Preferred Stock ranks pari passu with the Common Stock, has no right to receive dividends or distributions not payable to holders of Common Stock, and is otherwise substantially equivalent to the Common Stock, but does not have general voting rights. The IEP Preferred Stock will automatically convert into Common Stock on a one-for-one basis, subject to customary adjustments, promptly following expiration of the applicable waiting period under Rule 14c-2 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “ Exchange Act ”). The amount of the IEP Commitment will be automatically and permanently reduced, on a dollar-for-dollar basis, by the aggregate gross cash proceeds received by the Company from public offerings of its Common Stock consummated after the date of the IEP Subscription Agreement and before the funding of the IEP Commitment. The proceeds of the IEP Commitment, if needed, will be used solely to fund a portion of the consideration for the Acquisition. 
 
AEP’s obligation to consummate the IEP Commitment is subject to the concurrent satisfaction or waiver of the conditions to the closing of the Acquisition under the Purchase Agreement (other than conditions to be satisfied at closing, subject to their satisfaction or waiver at closing), the substantially concurrent consummation of the Acquisition and certain other customary closing conditions. The IEP Subscription Agreement will terminate in specified circumstances, including upon termination of the Purchase Agreement in accordance with its terms or receipt by the Company of aggregate gross cash proceeds of at least $500,000,000 from such public offerings. The Company has agreed to pay AEP a non-refundable commitment fee equal to 1.00% of the aggregate commitment under the IEP Subscription Agreement, which is payable upon execution of the IEP Subscription Agreement, and to reimburse AEP’s transaction expenses. The terms of the IEP Commitment, the IEP Subscription Agreement and the IEP Stockholders Agreement (as defined below) have been approved by the Company’s Board of Directors upon the recommendation of a special committee of the Company’s Board of Directors comprised entirely of independent and disinterested directors. 
 
 IEP Stockholders and Registration Rights Agreement 
 
In connection with the execution of the IEP Subscription Agreement, on November 2, 2026, the Company entered into a Stockholders and Registration Rights Agreement (the “ IEP Stockholders Agreement ”) with Icahn Enterprises L.P. (“IEP”). Pursuant to the IEP Stockholders Agreement, IEP is entitled to designate six directors to serve on the Board of Directors of the Company (the “ Board ”) while IEP and its controlled affiliates beneficially own more than 50% of the Company’s outstanding Common Stock and five directors while they own at least 40% but not more than 50% of the Company’s 

outstanding Common Stock. Below those thresholds, IEP may designate four, three, two or one directors based on adjusted ownership thresholds of 30%, 20%, 10% and 5%, respectively, with protections against dilution based upon share issuances by the Company and adjustments for changes in board size. Reductions in IEP Board designation rights will be determined based upon IEP’s ownership percentages immediately before the Company mails its proxy statement for the next annual meeting of the Company’s stockholders in any applicable year, and such reduction will take effect at the next annual meeting of the Company’s stockholders following reduction below the applicable threshold. The IEP Stockholders Agreement also provides, at IEP’s request, for at least one qualified IEP designee to serve on each standing committee of the Board, subject to applicable law, exchange independence requirements and specified conflict exceptions, as well as IEP’s consent over the adoption, amendment or maintenance of any stockholder rights plan by the Company. 
 
Pursuant to the IEP Stockholders Agreement, the Company will grant IEP and its affiliates certain demand, piggyback and shelf registration rights with respect to the Common Stock owned by IEP and its affiliates, subject to certain customary thresholds and conditions. In addition, under the IEP Stockholders Agreement, IEP and its affiliates may conduct an underwritten registered secondary offering of the Company’s Common Stock prior to the Acquisition Closing following or concurrently with the Company’s public offering of Shares in connection with the Acquisition, including shares sold pursuant to an underwriters’ option, on terms determined by IEP in its sole discretion. The Company has agreed to take all actions reasonably necessary to facilitate such offering. The Company will not receive any proceeds from the sale of such shares by IEP and its affiliates. 
 
IEP and its affiliates will also be entitled to information and preemptive rights for so long as they collectively retain at least 5% of the outstanding Common Stock of the Company, with certain protections against dilution based upon share issuances by the Company. 
 
The foregoing description does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the IEP Stockholders Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 10.3 to this Current Report and incorporated herein by reference.
