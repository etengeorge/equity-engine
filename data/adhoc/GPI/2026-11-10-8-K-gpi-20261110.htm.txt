 Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. 

 On November 10, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Group 1 Automotive, Inc. (the “Company”), upon the recommendation of the Company’s President and Chief Executive Officer, appointed Peter C. DeLongchamps, currently the Company’s Senior Vice President, Manufacturer Relations, Financial Services and Corporate Development, as President – U.S. Operations of the Company, effective November 10, 2026. In this role, Mr. DeLongchamps will report to the President and Chief Executive Officer and will have general supervision and control of the Company’s United States dealership operations. 

 Mr. DeLongchamps, age 65, has served as the Company’s Senior Vice President, Manufacturer Relations, Financial Services and Corporate Development since February 2026. He previously served as the Company’s Senior Vice President, Manufacturer Relations, Financial Services and Public Affairs from December 2017 through January 2026; Vice President, Manufacturer Relations, Financial Services and Public Affairs from January 2012 through December 2017; Vice President, Manufacturer Relations and Public Affairs from January 2006 through December 2011; and Vice President, Manufacturer Relations from July 2004 through December 2005. Immediately prior to joining the Company in 2004, he was President of Advantage BMW, a Houston-based automotive retailer. Mr. DeLongchamps began his automotive retailing career in 1980, having served as District Manager for General Motors Corporation and Regional Operations Manager for BMW of North America, as well as in various other management positions in the automotive industry. 

 In connection with his promotion, Mr. DeLongchamps’ annual base salary will be increased to $750,000 and his target annual incentive opportunity will be 90% of base salary. 

 There are no arrangements or understandings between Mr. DeLongchamps and any other person pursuant to which he was appointed as an officer. There are no family relationships between Mr. DeLongchamps and any director or executive officer of the Company, and Mr. DeLongchamps has no direct or indirect material interest in any transaction required to be disclosed pursuant to Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K. 

 Item 5.03 Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year. 

 On November 10, 2026, the Board adopted the Seventh Amended and Restated Bylaws of the Company (the “Amended Bylaws”), effective immediately. Among other things, the Amended Bylaws: 

 • provide that stockholders may request a special meeting only if they hold “Net Long Beneficial Ownership” of at least 25% of the outstanding common stock through the meeting date, excluding borrowed shares and including loaned shares recallable on five business days’ notice or less; 

 • revise the advance notice provisions to define “acting in concert” (with customary exclusions), limit the persons acting in concert treated as “Proposing Persons” to those known after reasonable inquiry, and require disclosure of agreements, arrangements and understandings relating to director nominations or the voting or holding of Company securities, rather than a summary of discussions with other stockholders; 

 • provide that whether a director election is contested will be determined as of the tenth day before the Company first mails its notice of meeting, and require the Board to act on a tendered director resignation within 90 days after certification of the election results; and 

 • make certain other procedural, technical, conforming and clarifying changes. 

 The foregoing description of the Amended Bylaws is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Amended Bylaws, which are attached as Exhibit 3.1 hereto and incorporated herein by reference.
