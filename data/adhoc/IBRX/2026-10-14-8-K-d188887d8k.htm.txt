 Item 7.01 
 
Regulation FD Disclosure. 

On October 14, 2026, ImmunityBio, Inc. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has expanded the approved indication for ANKTIVA ® (nogapendekin alfa inbakicept-pmln) in combination with Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) to include patients with BCG-unresponsive NMIBC with papillary-only tumors. The full text of the Company’s press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 hereto.
