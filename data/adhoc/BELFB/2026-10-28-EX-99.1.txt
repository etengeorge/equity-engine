 Bel Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results 

 Provides Q4-26 Sales and Gross Margin Guidance 

 WEST ORANGE, NJ , Wednesday , October 28, 2026 -- Bel Fuse Inc . (Nasdaq: BELFA and BELFB) ( “Bel” or the “Company” ) today announced preliminary financial results for the third quarter of 2026 . 

 Third Quarter 2026 Highlights 

 • 

 Net sales of $ 220.5 million compared to $179.0 million in Q3-25. Up 23.2% from Q3-25 

 • 

 Gross profit margin of 40.9% , up from 39.7% in Q3-25 

 • 
 GAAP net earnings attributable to Bel shareholders of $26.3 million in Q3-26, compared to net earnings of $22.3 million in Q3-25. Non-GAAP net earnings attributable to Bel shareholders of $44.0 million in Q3-26, versus $26.3 million in Q3-25 

 • 
 Adjusted EBITDA of $52.5 million (23.8% of sales), compared to $39.2 million (21.9% of sales) in Q3-25 

Farouq Tuweiq, President and CEO of Bel, said, “We are pleased with our strong third quarter results, with performance led by our Data Solutions sector, Industrials end market, and across our distribution partners. The quarter reflects momentum across key end markets, supported by focused execution across our global organization.”

“Looking ahead, we expect a strong fourth quarter, guiding net sales of $220 million to $240 million and gross margin of 40.0% to 42.0%. We are excited about how the year is shaping up and remain focused on serving our customers, executing our growth strategy, and creating long-term value for our shareholders,” concluded Mr. Tuweiq.

 About Bel 

 Bel (www.belfuse.com) designs, manufactures, and markets critical electronic components, systems and solutions for customers in aerospace, defense, industrial, and data-driven markets. Understanding that our customers face increasingly complex technical challenges, Bel delivers a comprehensive portfolio of solutions including power systems, high-reliability connectors and cable assemblies, circuit protection, and networking products that enable Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) to bring their innovations to market. Bel partners closely with customers to deliver both customized and standard solutions tailored to their specific applications and performance requirements. With manufacturing facilities and technical support teams worldwide, Bel serves as a strategic partner to customers who require proven reliability in demanding end markets.

 Non-GAAP Financial Measures 

The Non-GAAP financial measures identified in this press release as well as in the supplementary information to this press release (Non-GAAP net earnings attributable to Bel shareholders, Non-GAAP EPS, Non-GAAP Operating Income, Non-GAAP Provision for Income Taxes and Adjusted EBITDA) are not measures of performance under accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America ("GAAP"). These measures should not be considered a substitute for, and the reader should also consider, income from operations, provision for income taxes, net earnings, earnings per share and other measures of performance as defined by GAAP as indicators of our performance or profitability. Our non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to other similarly-titled captions of other companies due to differences in the method of calculation. With these measures we present results adjusted to exclude the effects of certain unusual or special items that occur outside the ordinary course of business and are not used by management for evaluating operating performance, as well their related tax impact, all of which would otherwise be included under U.S. GAAP. We believe that these non-GAAP measures of financial results provide useful information to management and investors regarding certain financial and business trends relating to our financial condition and results of operations. We use these non-GAAP measures to compare the Company’s performance to that of prior periods for trend analysis and for budgeting and planning purposes. We also believe that the use of these non-GAAP financial measures provides an additional tool for investors to use in evaluating ongoing operating results and trends and in comparing the Company’s financial measures with other similarly situated companies in our industry, many of which present similar non-GAAP financial measures to investors. Non-GAAP financial measures, such as Non-GAAP net earnings attributable to Bel shareholders, Non-GAAP EPS, Non-GAAP Operating Income, Non-GAAP Provision for Income Taxes and Adjusted EBITDA, adjust corresponding GAAP measures for provision for income taxes, other income/expense, net, interest income/expense, and depreciation and amortization, and also exclude, where applicable for the covered period presented in the financial statements, certain unusual or special items identified by management as discussed above, such as stock-based compensation, amortization of inventory step-up, amortization of intangibles (which primarily related to the amortization of finite-lived customer relationships and technology associated with the company's historical acquisitions), unrealized foreign currency exchange (gains) losses, restructuring charges (credits), gains/losses on sales of businesses and properties (including both non-operational properties in Hong Kong and operational properties elsewhere), acquisition related costs (for proposed or completed transactions), earnout liability adjustments, impairment charges, noncontrolling interest ("NCI") adjustments from fair value to redemption value, write-off of deferred financing costs, and certain litigation costs. Please refer to the financial information included with this press release for reconciliations of GAAP financial measures to Non-GAAP financial measures.
