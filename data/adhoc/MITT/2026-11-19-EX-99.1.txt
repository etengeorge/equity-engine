 TPG Mortgage Investment Trust, Inc. Provides Update to Shareholders 

 NEW YORK, NY - Business Wire - November 19, 2026 - TPG Mortgage Investment Trust, Inc. ("MITT," "we," the "Company," or "our") (NYSE: MITT) announced today an update on its portfolio and liquidity position, including certain estimated financial information as of October 31, 2026. 

 In light of sustained market volatility and to enhance transparency to shareholders, the Company has elected to provide the following updates on its business and financial performance: 

 Book Value Per Common Share (1)(2) : The Company estimates that Book Value per share as of October 31, 2026 was between $9.20 and $9.30, as compared to $9.27 per share as of September 30, 2026 

 Investment Portfolio (3) : The Company’s Investment Portfolio as of October 31, 2026 is estimated to be $7.5 billion as compared to $7.6 billion as of September 30, 2026 

 Financing (3) : The Company's total financing is estimated to be $7.2 billion as of October 31, 2026, consisting of $6.1 billion of non-recourse financing and $1.1 billion of recourse financing, consistent with $6.1 billion and $1.1 billion, respectively, as of September 30, 2026 

 Leverage: The Company’s GAAP Leverage Ratio and Economic Leverage Ratio (4) as of October 31, 2026 is estimated to be consistent with 13.7x and 2.0x, respectively, as of September 30, 2026 

 Liquidity (5) : The Company’s total liquidity as of October 31, 2026 is estimated to be $84.6 million 

 The financial information set forth above is unaudited and reflects the Company's estimates with respect to such information, based on information currently available to management, and may vary materially from the Company's actual financial results as of the periods noted above. Further, these estimates are not a comprehensive statement or estimate of the Company's financial results or financial condition. These estimates should not be viewed as a substitute for financial statements prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP, and they are not necessarily indicative of the results to be achieved in the three-month period or year ending December 31, 2026 or any future period. 

 These estimates, which are the responsibility of the Company's management, were prepared by the Company's management and are based upon a number of assumptions. Additional items that may require adjustments to these estimates may be identified and could result in material changes to these estimates. These estimates are inherently uncertain and the Company undertakes no obligation to update or revise this information, including to provide similar financial or portfolio updates in the future. Accordingly, a reader should not place undue reliance on these estimates. 

 ABOUT TPG MORTGAGE INVESTMENT TRUST, INC. 

 TPG Mortgage Investment Trust, Inc. is a residential mortgage REIT with a focus on investing in a diversified risk-adjusted portfolio of residential mortgage-related assets in the U.S. mortgage market. The Company is externally managed and advised by AG REIT Management, LLC, an affiliate of TPG Inc. (NASDAQ: TPG). 

 Additional information can be found on the Company’s website at www.mitt.tpg.com.

 (3) The Investment Portfolio consists of Residential Investments, Agency RMBS, and Legacy WMC Commercial Investments, all of which are held at fair value. Financing is inclusive of Securitized Debt, which is held at fair value, Financing Arrangements, and Senior Unsecured Notes. This press release excludes investments held through AG Arc LLC unless otherwise noted. 

 (4) We define GAAP Leverage as the sum of (1) Securitized debt, at fair value, (2) Financing arrangements, net of any restricted cash posted on such financing arrangements, (3) Senior Unsecured Notes, and (4) the amount payable on purchases that have not yet settled less the financing remaining on sales that have not yet settled. We define Economic Leverage, a non-GAAP financial measure, as the sum of our GAAP Leverage, exclusive of any fully non-recourse financing arrangements, and our net TBA position (at cost), if any. Our leverage does not include any financing utilized through AG Arc. 

 (5) Total liquidity includes $57.3 million of cash and cash equivalents and $27.3 million of available committed financing. As of October 31, 2026, we pledged residential mortgage loans in which the Company has no outstanding financing but has $27.3 million of available financing which is contractually committed.
