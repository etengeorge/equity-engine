 Item 8.01 Other Events. 

 Common Stock Dividend 

 On October 28, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors declared the Company’s quarterly dividend of $0.15 per share of common stock outstanding. The dividend is expected to be paid on November 25, 2026, to stockholders of record at the close of business on November 16, 2026. A copy of the news release announcing the quarterly dividend is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference. 

 Share Repurchase Program 

 In addition, the Company today announced that its Board of Directors has approved the renewal and increase of its share repurchase program under which the Company may repurchase its common stock for an aggregate purchase price of up to $200.0 million (the “Share Repurchase Program”). Share repurchases may be made at management’s discretion from time to time in the open market (either with or without a 10b5-1 plan) or through privately negotiated transactions. The actual number of shares of common stock repurchased, and the timing and price of any repurchases, will depend on market conditions, applicable legal requirements and other factors. The Company is not obligated to acquire any particular number of shares of common stock. The Share Repurchase Program has been extended through December 31, 2031, but also may be suspended, modified or discontinued at any time.
