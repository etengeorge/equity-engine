 Item 1.01 Entry into Material Definitive Agreement 

 On August 3, 2026, Willis Lease Finance Corporation (the “Company”) executed a Purchase and Sale Agreement to purchase three commercial buildings, totaling 375,000 square feet, located in Coconut Creek, Florida for $118.0 million. The Company intends to use the acquired properties to support its expanding operations, including its corporate headquarters, facilities for its spare parts business, maintenance repair, and overhaul services, as well as space for preservation and engine storage. Closing of the acquisition is subject to customary closing conditions.
