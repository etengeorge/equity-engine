RISKS
 
Kurdikar stated, "The year-over-year decline in Q3 was slightly greater than in Q2," regarding the rate of decline in total prescriptions for insulin pens in the retail channel.
 
SUMMARY

Management reported significant sequential improvements in revenue and operating income, driven by U.S. and International performance and the mid-quarter closure of the Owen Mumford acquisition. The company raised its full-year guidance for adjusted operating margin and earnings per share while reaffirming its top-line revenue targets. Strategic efforts remained focused on integrating the new pharmaceutical services business, completing the global brand transition, and advancing regulatory reviews for market-appropriate injection devices.
 
Kurdikar noted integration of Owen Mumford is "progressing as planned" and expands the addressable auto-injector market to approximately $2.4 billion.

Management established a new pharma services organization led by Jeff Mann to focus on auto-injector platforms and pharmaceutical industry partnerships.

The company completed brand transitions in major European and Asian markets, with more than 90% of revenue now derived from products under the **Embecta Corp.** ( EMBC +3.35% ) label.

B2B co-packaging momentum continued with generic GLP-1 therapies featuring the company's pen needles launched in Canada, Brazil, and South Africa.

Elguicze highlighted that a revised cost structure review led to raised margin guidance, with further benefits expected to materialize in fiscal year 2027.

CEO Kurdikar identified "meaningful opportunities to enhance commercial execution" in the U.S. following an assessment by the new North American president.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY
 
 Aidaptus : A next-generation auto-injector platform designed to accommodate various fill volumes and simplify supply chain logistics.

 B2B : Business-to-business, referring here to co-packaging agreements where the company's needles are bundled with other pharmaceutical products.

 CE Mark : A certification mark that indicates conformity with health, safety, and environmental protection standards for products sold within the European Economic Area.

 GLP-1 : Glucagon-like peptide-1, a class of drugs used to treat type 2 diabetes and obesity, often delivered via pen injectors.

 Pen Needle : A specialized needle used in conjunction with injection pens to deliver medications like insulin or GLP-1s.

 SOFR : Secured Overnight Financing Rate, a broad measure of the cost of borrowing cash overnight collateralized by Treasury securities.

 TRx : Total prescriptions, a measure of the volume of prescriptions filled in the retail pharmacy channel.
