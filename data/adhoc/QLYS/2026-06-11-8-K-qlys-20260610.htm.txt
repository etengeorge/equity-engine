 Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. 

 On June 10, 2026, the Company held its 2026 annual meeting of stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). Present at the Annual Meeting in person or by proxy were holders of 31,972,103 shares of the Company’s common stock, or approximately 90.60% of the shares outstanding and entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting.
