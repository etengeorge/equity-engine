 Vistance Networks Board Approves Special Distribution 

 Richardson, TX, October 14, 2026 – Vistance Networks (NASDAQ: VISN) (“Vistance” or the “Company”), a global provider of intelligent network solutions, today announced its Board of Directors (the “Board”) approved a special cash distribution of $1.50 per share, payable on November 6, 2026, to holders of record of its common stock as of the close of business on October 26, 2026. 

 Pursuant to Nasdaq Rule 11140(b)(2), because the amount of the special cash distribution will be 25% or greater of the value of the Company’s common stock, the ex-dividend date will be November 9, 2026, the first business day following the payment date. 

 The Company expects to fund the special cash distribution with available cash, including proceeds from the July 1, 2026 sale of its Ruckus Networks business. 

 “We are pleased to return an additional $1.50 per share to our shareholders, bringing our total special distributions this year to $16.50 per share,” said Chuck Treadway, president and chief executive officer of Vistance Networks. “This distribution, together with the other special distributions this year and our ongoing share repurchase program, reflects our commitment to returning capital to shareholders. We remain focused on disciplined capital allocation while advancing Aurora Networks’ strategic plan and pursuing opportunities to create shareholder value.”
