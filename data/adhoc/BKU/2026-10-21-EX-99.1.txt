 BankUnited, Inc. Reports 3Q 2026 Net Income of $72 million, $1.01 Diluted EPS; 

 Excluding Impact of Securities Sales, Net Income of $74 million, $1.03 Diluted EPS 

 Continued Franchise Momentum Drives NIM Expansion, Stronger Funding Mix and Improved Credit Trends 

 Miami Lakes, Fla. — October 21, 2026 — BankUnited, Inc. (the “Company”) (NYSE: BKU) today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. 

 Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer Rajinder Singh commented, "We delivered another quarter of solid performance, with continued deposit momentum and net interest margin expanding to 3.16%, ahead of our guidance. Benefiting from a strong economy, we remain focused on increasing profitability and creating long-term shareholder value."

 Performance Highlights 
 • Net Interest Margin (NIM) of 3.16%, up 16 bps from a year ago and up 10 bps from prior quarter. 

 • Diluted EPS of $1.01 , up $0.06 from a year ago and $0.04 from prior quarter. 

 • Adjusted Diluted EPS 1, 2 of $1.03 , up $0.09 from a year ago and $0.07 from prior quarter. 

 • Approximately 2.1 million shares repurchased in Q3 for an aggregate of $97.0 million . 

 Deposits 
 • Non-Interest Demand Deposits (NIDDA): 

 ◦ Average NIDDA up $824 million , or 10% , from a year ago and remained stable from prior quarter, continuing to serve as a key funding source despite ending balance fluctuations. 

 ◦ Ending NIDDA up $488 million , or 6% , from a year ago and down $821 million , or 8% , from prior quarter primarily due to seasonal normalization following elevated inflows in Q2. 

 ◦ Represents 32.2% of total deposits, up from 30.1% a year ago. 

 • Average Total Deposits (excluding brokered) : Up $1.0 billion from a year ago and down $385 million from prior quarter. 

 Loans 
 • Average Core Loans increased $706 million , or 4% , from a year ago and decreased $147 million, or 1% , from prior quarter. 

 • Total Average Loans remained stable from a year ago and decreased by $314 million from the prior quarter due to continued purposeful runoff in non-core loans. 

 Credit 
 • Criticized and Classified Loans decreased $176 million , or 14% , from a year ago and decreased $10 million , or 1% , from prior quarter. 

 • Net Charge-Offs ratio remained low, 0.11%, in both the current and prior quarter, and down from 0.25% a year ago. 

 • NPLs down $116 million, or 30% , from a year ago, and up $40 million , or 18% , from the prior quarter.

 Net interest income and margin increased from the prior quarter due to the following factors: 

 • Deposit pricing continued to improve, contributing to lower funding costs; average cost of deposits improved by 6 bps to 1.99% . 

 • The tax equivalent yield on investment securities benefited from the sale of lower-yielding securities and reinvestment into higher-yielding securities, which improved the overall yield of the investment portfolio. 

 • Tax equivalent yields on commercial loans increased 5 bps from the prior quarter, reflecting favorable loan repricing and improved commercial loan portfolio mix. 

 Net interest income and margin also increased from a year ago due to the following factors: 

 • Average NIDDA grew by $824 million while average interest bearing liabilities declined by $1.0 billion ; average cost of deposits improved by 39 bps from a year ago. 

 • Partially offset by lower tax-equivalent asset yields, as variable-rate loans and securities repriced downward faster than the benefit from lower funding costs and favorable funding mix dynamic due to lower SOFR/Fed funds basis.

 Credit Quality 

 Credit quality metrics remained strong during Q3, as criticized and classified loans decreased and non-performing loans modestly increased from prior quarter. 

 • Criticized and Classified Loans: Decreased $10 million from prior quarter, and $176 million from a year ago. 

 • Net Charge-Offs: Net charge-offs for the quarter (annualized) was 0.11%, flat from prior quarter and down 0.14% from a year ago. 

 • Non-Performing Loans: Up $40 million , or 18% , from prior quarter and down $116 million, or 30%, from a year ago. 

 • NPA Ratio: 0.77% , including 0.09% related to guaranteed portion of SBA loans, up from 0.66% , including 0.09% related to SBA, in prior quarter, and down from 1.10% , including 0.11% related to SBA, from a year ago.
