 ITEM 2.02 RESULTS OF OPERATIONS AND FINANCIAL CONDITION. 

 On July 28, 2026, Herc Holdings Inc. (the “Company”) issued a press release regarding its financial results for its second quarter ended June 30, 2026. A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Form 8-K. 

 On July 28, 2026, the Company will conduct an earnings webcast relating to the Company’s financial results for the second quarter of 2026. The earnings webcast will be made available to the public via a link on the Investor Relations section of the Company's website, IR.HercRentals.com, as well as via telephone dial-in, and the slides that will accompany the presentation will be available to the public at the time of the earnings webcast through the Company’s website.
