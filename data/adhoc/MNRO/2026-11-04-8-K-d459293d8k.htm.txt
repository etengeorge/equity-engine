 Item 1.01 
 
Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. 

On November 4, 2026, Monro, Inc. (the “ Company ”) entered into the Amendment to Rights Agreement (the “ Amendment ”) by and between the Company and Equiniti Trust Company, LLC, as rights agent (the “ Rights Agent ”), that amends the Rights Agreement, dated as of November 10, 2025 (the “ Rights Agreement ”), by and between the Company and the Rights Agent. All capitalized terms used herein and not otherwise defined herein shall have the meaning(s) ascribed to them in the Rights Agreement. 
 
The Amendment extends the final expiration date from 5:00 p.m., Eastern Time, on November 6, 2026 to 5:00 p.m., Eastern Time, on November 1, 2027. As a result of the Amendment, the Rights will expire at 5:00 p.m., Eastern Time, on November 1, 2027, unless the Rights are earlier redeemed, exchanged in full or terminated. 
 
The Company’s Board of Directors (the “ Board ”) determined to extend the expiration of the Rights Agreement to support the Company’s ongoing review of strategic alternatives. The extension is intended to preserve the Board’s ability to conduct the review effectively and to protect the interests of the Company and its shareholders during the review. 
 
The foregoing description of the Amendment does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Amendment, attached hereto as Exhibit 4.2 and incorporated herein by reference.
