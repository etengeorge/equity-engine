 Ondas Acquires Three Defense Technology Businesses
to Expand Its Integrated Autonomous Defense Systems Platform 

 Acquisitions add field-deployed and near-term mission-ready
capabilities across acoustic intelligence, resilient communications, remote operations and GNSS-denied navigation, strengthening existing
Ondas platforms and enabling new integrated solutions and revenue opportunities. 

 Insignito adds a unique passive acoustic sensing
layer to detect, classify, and track drones particularly useful for small FPV drone systems and fiber optic systems that are difficult
to detect by traditional methods. 

 Ottopia Defense combines AI-powered autonomy capabilities
and ultra-low-latency teleoperation, and intelligent networking, connecting unmanned maneuvering platforms with human operators and command
centers for one-to-many capabilities. 

 Caribou Labs is a provider of GNSS-denied navigation
and resilient communications technology that will add key capabilities and differentiation for Ondas platforms in combat environments. 

 WEST PALM BEACH, FL / September 23, 2026 / Ondas
Inc. (Nasdaq: ONDS) (“Ondas” or the “Company”), a leading provider of autonomous systems and next-generation defense
and security technologies, announced today that it has acquired three defense technology businesses, adding advanced capabilities that
enhance the Company’s mission capabilities and expand its addressable markets. The
acquired businesses bring technologies ranging from systems already deployed in active conflict environments to capabilities positioned
for near-term mission integration across Ondas’ existing platforms. The
acquis itions are designed to strengthen Ondas’ existing and future autonomous platforms, accelerate the development of integrated
solutions and create new opportunities for growth across the defense and autonomous systems markets.

“These acquisitions reflect our strategy of
assembling the critical technologies required to make autonomous systems more capable, resilient and scalable in contested environments,”
said Eric Brock, Chairman and CEO of Ondas Inc. “Together, these businesses give Ondas more to sell and more ways to win. They add
commercial revenue streams we can grow across our customer base,
 and they strengthen our ability to pursue larger, longer - term
customer programs where we deploy and sustain more capable autonomous systems on behalf of the customers
we serve.”

 Insignito – AI-Enabled Acoustic Intelligence
for C-UAS. 

Insignito’s mission-ready technology creates
a passive acoustic sensing layer designed to detect, classify, track and localize drones by their acoustic signatures, including small
drones, FPV systems, low-altitude threats and autonomous or RF-silent platforms that can challenge traditional radar and radio-frequency
detection. Its flagship DUMBO system has been successfully deployed in conflict zones listening to the operating environment rather than
transmitting RF or electromagnetic energy, applying real-time signal processing, advanced acoustic modeling, artificial intelligence and
proprietary algorithms to identify unmanned aerial vehicle signatures. The technology is designed to complement radar, RF and electro-optical
sensors, adding an independent sensing modality to a layered, multi-sensor C-UAS architecture.

Beyond passive detection, Insignito has developed
several additional capabilities including WaveGuide, a software-based mission-planning capability that models the acoustic detectability
of aerial platforms along planned trajectories; Silent Propellers, which applies acoustic engineering to reduce the noise signature of
unmanned aircraft; and Banshee, an acoustic-enabled drone interception technology under development. Collectively, these capabilities
span detection, mission planning, signature reduction and interception.

 Ottopia Defense – Mission-Critical Communications
and Remote Control for Unmanned Systems .

Ottopia Defense (“Ottoppia”) provides
software and communications infrastructure connecting unmanned maneuvering platforms with remote operators and command centers. The technology
is designed to maintain the continuous, real-time flow of video, telemetry, mission data and commands in remote, harsh, bandwidth-constrained,
degraded and mission-critical environments. Its architecture dynamically manages high-priority operational data across available communications
channels, helping preserve the connection between unmannedplatforms and human operators when battlefield connectivity is inconsistent
or disrupted.

Ottopia’s mission-ready platform combines AI-enabled
autonomy, ultra-low-latency teleoperation, remote control and resilient communications, enabling operators to supervise multiple unmanned
systems, monitor live video and telemetry, and intervene whenever human judgment or direct control is required. Its one-to-many architecture
allows a smaller number of operators to manage multiple assets from centralized mission centers or distributed command environments while
preserving human oversight and command authority.

 Caribou Labs – Resilient Communications for
GNSS-denied environments. 

Caribou Labs (“Caribou”) is a deep-tech,
dual-use company specializing in EW environments providing resilient communications and deterministic identification. The company combines
domain expertise in RF engineering, sensing and defense systems. Caribou’s mission is to deliver GPS-independent positioning timing
and visibility when digital infrastructure fails. The company’s products are complementary to Ondas’ autonomous systems by
helping to complete an end-to-end autonomy and resiliency stack that includes connectivity, navigation, identification and regulatory
compliance across aerial, ground and command platforms. Caribou’s technology is currently deployed in active conflict environments
and has a global pipeline of new customer opportunities.

 Building an Integrated Technology Stack for Autonomous
Defense 

Ondas intends to integrate these technologies across
its existing portfolio. Insignito adds acoustic sensing that enhances Ondas’ current C-UAS solution with an additional FPV drone
detection layer. Ottopia provides the resilient connectivity and human command capabilities required for remote operations of unmanned
ground vehicles such as Iron Wave. Caribou adds navigation, positioning and communications capabilities for GNSS-denied environments that
are required across Ondas’ platforms. Combined with Ondas’ existing autonomous platforms, sensors, software and mission systems,
these capabilities are expected to strengthen an increasingly integrated technology stack spanning detection, connectivity, navigation,
autonomy and mission execution. This approach is central to Ondas’ Systems-of-Systems strategy: combining differentiated technologies
across multiple platforms to enhance existing products, accelerate the development of new solutions and expand the markets and revenue
opportunities available to the Company.

 Transaction Summary of Strategic Acquisitions 

Ondas acquired the businesses for aggregate consideration
of $56 million, payable in cash or Ondas common stock. Based on Ondas’ forecasts, the purchase price represents less than three
times the businesses’ expected 2027 revenue. The transactions also include potential earnout consideration of up to $32 million
in the aggregate, payable in cash or Ondas common stock upon the achievement of key performance milestones through 2028.

 In
connection with the acquisitions, the Company approved inducement grants of restricted stock units (RSUs) representing 2,979,063
shares of the Company’s common stock and stock options exercisable for
80,000 shares of the Company’s common stock with an exercise price of $7.72 per
share to a total of 37 newly-hired employees in connection with the acquisitions. The equity awards were granted under the Ondas Inc.
2026 Inducement Plan pursuant to the Nasdaq Rule 5635(c)(4) inducement grant exception as a component of each individual’s employment
compensation and were granted as an inducement material to his or her acceptance of employment with the Company. RSUs representing (i)
 222,727 shares of the Company’s common stock vest in eight (8) equal
quarterly installments over two years following the closing date, subject to the applicable employee’s continued employment with
the Company; (ii) 1,866,061 shares of the Company’s common stock
vest one-third on September 23, 2027 and subsequently in eight equal quarterly installments, subject to the applicable employee’s
continued employment with the Company; and (iii) 890,275 shares of the
Company’s common stock vest on the closing date. Stock options representing 80,000 shares of the Company’s common stock vest
one-third on September 23, 2027 and subsequently in twenty-four equal monthly installments, subject to the applicable employee’s
continued employment with the Company.
