 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE 

 NICOLET BANKSHARES, INC. ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 EARNINGS 

 • Net income of $64 million ($68 million core*) for third quarter 2026, compared to net income of $57 million ($65 million core*) for second quarter 2026 

 • Diluted earnings per share of $2.96 ($3.15 core*) for third quarter 2026, compared to $2.62 ($2.99 core*) for second quarter 2026 

 • Completed the MidWestOne integration and core systems conversion, the sale of the Denver branches, and moved both wealth management platforms to a common operating system 

 • Net interest margin expanded to 4.19% for third quarter 2026, compared to 4.14% for second quarter 2026 

 * Core net income and core diluted earnings per share are non-GAAP financial measures 

 Green Bay, Wisconsin, October 20, 2026 - Nicolet Bankshares, Inc. (NYSE: NIC) (“Nicolet”) announced net income of $64 million and earnings per diluted common share of $2.96 for third quarter 2026, compared to net income of $57 million and earnings per diluted common share of $2.62 for second quarter 2026, and net income of $42 million and earnings per diluted common share of $2.73 for third quarter 2025. Net income included certain non-core items, mostly merger-related expenses, that negatively impacted earnings per diluted common share $0.19 for third quarter 2026 and $0.37 for second quarter 2026, resulting in core diluted earnings per common share (non-GAAP) of $3.15 and $2.99, respectively. 

 “Our third quarter results are beginning to show what this combined company can produce,” said Mike Daniels, Chairman, President, and CEO of Nicolet. “Core earnings increased, our net interest margin expanded again, and we delivered a core return on average tangible common equity of 22%. Our people produced those results all while completing the MidWest One systems conversion and integration, closing the Denver branch sale, and bringing our teams together around a shared culture. That takes a lot of work. I am proud of how they handled it while continuing to do the thousand little things that matter to our customers and communities every day.” 

 Daniels continued, “Core deposits grew $223 million, our funding mix improved, and asset quality remained solid. Our strong profitability also supported $40 million in common stock repurchases. We expect a cleaner fourth quarter giving a clearer view of what this company can earn as we head into 2027. From here, our focus is on growing customer relationships, and staying disciplined on credit and expenses. We like where we are headed. Now we need to keep delivering.” 

 Nicolet’s financial performance and certain balance sheet line items were impacted by the timing and size of the MidWest One Financial Group, Inc. (“MidWest One ”) acquisition on February 13, 2026. Certain income statement results, average balances, and related ratios for 2026 include partial contributions from MidWest One from the acquisition date. At acquisition, MidWest One added total assets of $6.1 billion, loans of $4.4 billion, and deposits of $5.3 billion.

 Balance Sheet Review 

 At September 30, 2026, period end assets were $15.1 billion, a decrease of $350 million from June 30, 2026, largely due to the Denver branches divestiture that closed on July 28, 2026. Total loans increased $34 million from June 30, 2026, while securities available for sale decreased $26 million. Total deposits of $12.6 billion at September 30, 2026, increased $73 million from June 30, 2026, including a $223 million increase in core deposits, partly offset by a $150 million decrease in brokered deposits. Total capital was $2.2 billion at September 30, 2026, a decrease of $37 million from June 30, 2026, with earnings more than offset by unfavorable market valuations on the investment portfolio from recent interest rate increases, common stock repurchases, and the quarterly common stock dividend. 

 Asset Quality 

 Nonperforming assets were $78 million and represented 0.52% of total assets at September 30, 2026, compared to $75 million (0.49% of total assets) at June 30, 2026. The allowance for credit losses-loans was $135 million and represented 1.24% of total loans at September 30, 2026, compared to $134 million (or 1.23% of total loans) at June 30, 2026. Asset quality trends remain solid and loan net charge-offs were negligible. 

 Income Statement Review - Quarter 

 Net income was $64 million for third quarter 2026, compared to net income of $57 million for second quarter 2026. 

 Net interest income was $142 million for third quarter 2026, $1 million (1%) higher than second quarter 2026. Average interest-earning assets of $13.6 billion were down $226 million from second quarter 2026, mostly due to $263 million lower average loans related to the Denver branches sale, partly offset by organic growth. Average interest-bearing liabilities of $10.0 billion were down $388 million from second quarter 2026, primarily due to $321 million lower average interest-bearing deposits also attributable to the Denver branches sale. 

 The net interest margin for third quarter 2026 was 4.19%, compared to 4.14% for second quarter 2026. The yield on interest-earning assets increased 2 bps (to 5.88%), including a 6 bps increase in loan yield (to 6.32%) partly offset by a 4 bps decrease in investment yield from lower discount accretion related to the early call of a municipal bond in second quarter 2026. On the funding side, the cost of interest-bearing liabilities for third quarter 2026 increased 1 bp (to 2.30%). 

 Noninterest income was $32 million for third quarter 2026, down $4 million compared to second quarter 2026, largely due to unfavorable market valuations. Net asset gains (losses) were $3 million unfavorable and deferred compensation plan asset market valuations were $2 million unfavorable from second quarter 2026 to third quarter 2026, mostly due to changes in market valuations. Excluding net asset gains (losses) and the deferred compensation plan asset market valuations, noninterest income increased $1 million, primarily due to an increase in wealth management fee income. 

 Noninterest expense was $91 million for third quarter 2026, compared to $104 million for second quarter 2026. Personnel expense decreased $4 million from second quarter 2026, reflecting lower salaries from the decline in average full-time equivalent employees, as well as the $2 million offsetting market valuation for the deferred compensation plan liabilities. Non-personnel expenses decreased $9 million from second quarter 2026, primarily due to a $3 million reduction in merger-related expense and a $5 million reduction in other noninterest expense for second quarter loss on the early redemption of junior subordinated debentures. 

 Declaration of Quarterly Cash Dividend to Shareholders 

 On October 19, 2026, Nicolet’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.36 per share to holders of its common stock. The dividend is payable on December 15, 2026, to shareholders of record as of December 1, 2026. 

 Next Quarterly Earnings Release 

 Nicolet expects to issue the fourth quarter and full year 2026 earnings release on January 19, 2027. 

 About Nicolet Bankshares, Inc. 

 Nicolet Bankshares, Inc. is the bank holding company of Nicolet National Bank, a growing, full-service, community bank providing services ranging from commercial, agricultural and consumer banking to wealth management and retirement plan services. Founded in Green Bay in 2000, Nicolet National Bank operates branches primarily in Wisconsin, Iowa, Michigan, and Minnesota. More information can be found at www.nicoletbank.com.
