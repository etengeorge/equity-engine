 Item 8.01 Other Events. 

On April 1, 2026, Lands’ End, Inc. (the “Company”)
announced that its Board of Directors has authorized the Company to repurchase up to $100 million of the Company’s common stock
through March 31, 2029. Under the program, the Company may repurchase its common stock through open market purchases, in privately negotiated
transactions, or by other means in accordance with applicable federal securities laws, including Rule 10b-18 of the Exchange Act. The
timing of purchases and the exact number of shares to be purchased will be determined by the Company’s management, in its discretion,
or pursuant to a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan, and will depend upon market conditions and other factors. The share repurchase program may
be suspended or discontinued at any time. The Company issued a press release announcing the share repurchase authorization on April 1,
2026, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1.
