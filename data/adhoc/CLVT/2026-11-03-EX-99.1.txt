 Clarivate Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results, Delivering Clear Path to Value Creation 

 — Improved recurring organic revenue growth driven by portfolio transformation 

 and AI-led innovation — 

 — Updates 2026 financial outlook following the divestiture of Life Sciences & Healthcare segment — 

 London, UK -- November 3, 2026 Clarivate Plc (NYSE: CLVT) (the “Company” or “Clarivate”), a leading 

 global provider of transformative intelligence, today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 

 2026 . 

 Executive Commentary 

 Matti Shem Tov, Chief Executive Officer: 

 “Third quarter results continued to improve across key metrics, including organic recurring revenue growth and 

 the return to positive ACV growth in the Intellectual Property segment. We also achieved a major milestone with 

 the early close of the Life Sciences & Healthcare segment divestiture, positioning us for enhanced value creation 

 in our two market-leading segments, Academia & Government and Intellectual Property. The growing 

 commercial traction and customer validation from our AI-led innovation within A&G and IP give us confidence 

 in our ability to deliver on our commitments of accelerating sustained organic growth, expanding free cash flow 

 and reducing leverage.” 

 Michael Easton, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer: 

 “We are seeing the benefits of disciplined execution across the business, with improving growth trends, strong 

 margins and continued free cash flow generation. Our focus remains on translating organic growth into 

 sustainable earnings and free cash flow, while maintaining a disciplined approach to capital allocation. Looking 

 ahead, we are well positioned to further strengthen our financial profile and enhance our financial flexibility.” 

 Third Quarter 2026 Results 

 Total revenues were $567.9 million, including the Life Sciences & Healthcare segment (“LS&H”), which was 

 divested on October 6, 2026, compared to total revenues of $623.1 million for the third quarter of 2025, primarily 

 due to inorganic disposals . Excluding LS&H, which is now presented as discontinued operations, total 

 revenues were $481.6 million , compared to total revenues of $530.3 million for the third quarter of 2025 , 

 primarily due to inorganic disposals . 

 Organic revenues for continuing operations increased 0.4% as organic subscription growth of 1.5% and organic 

 re-occurring growth of 0.7% was partially offset by lower organic transactional revenues. 

 Organic ACV for continuing operations grew 1.8% compared to September 30, 2025 , reflecting continued 

 progress toward a more sustainable, subscription-led revenue base. 

 Net loss , including LS&H, was $80.8 million , or $0.13 per diluted share, compared to a net loss of $28.3 million , 

 or $0.04 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025 , driven by a $62.2 million non-cash LS&H goodwill 

 impairment charge . Adjusted net income, including LS&H, was $136.0 million , or $0.21 per diluted share, 

 compared to $119.3 million , or $0.18 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025 . Adjusted EBITDA, 

 including LS&H, was $245.3 million , compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $252.4 million for the third quarter of 

 2025 . 

 First Nine Months 2026 Results 

 Total revenues were $1,740.7 million , including the LS&H segment, compared to total revenues of $1,838.2 

 million , primarily due to inorganic disposals . Excluding the LS&H segment, which is now presented as 

 discontinued operations, total revenues were $1,472.4 million , compared to total revenues of $1,546.7 million 

 for the first nine months of 2025 , primarily due to inorganic disposals . 

 Organic revenues for continuing operations increased 0.1% , as a 0.9% increase in organic recurring revenues 

 (subscription and re-occurring) was offset by lower organic transactional revenues. 

 2 

 Net loss , including LS&H, was $389.6 million , or $0.61 per diluted share, compared to a net loss of $204.2 

 million , or $0.30 per diluted share, for the first nine months of 2025 , driven by a $283.9 million non-cash LS&H 

 goodwill impairment charge . Adjusted net income, including LS&H, was $378.4 million , or $0.59 per diluted 

 share, compared to $338.4 million , or $0.49 per diluted share, for the first nine months of 2025 . Adjusted 

 EBITDA, including LS&H, was $733.7 million , compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $747.2 million for the first 

 nine months of 2025 . 

 Clarivate, including LS&H, generated $399.0 million of operating cash flow and $233.1 million of free cash flow 

 during the first nine months of 2026 .

 Third Quarter 2026 Continuing Operations Commentary 

 Subscription revenues increased $2.4 million , or 0.7% , to $338.3 million . Organic subscription revenues increased 

 1.5% , primarily due to new sales and pricing actions. 

 Re-occurring revenues increased $0.5 million , or 0.5% , to $105.8 million . Organic re-occurring revenues 

 increased 0.7% , primarily due to higher IP volumes. 

 Recurring revenues, which consist of subscription and re-occurring revenues, increased 1.3% organically, or $5.7 

 million . 

 Transactional revenues decreased $51.6 million to $37.5 million , primarily due to product group wind-downs 

 within A&G. On an organic basis, the decline was 12.4% , or $3.9 million , primarily due to lower activity across 

 both segments, driven in part by customer migration to higher margin subscription products as we focus on our 

 recurring revenues. 

 Balance Sheet and Cash Flow 

 As of September 30, 2026 , cash and cash equivalents of $257.0 million decreased $72.2 million compared to 

 December 31, 2025 . 

 Total debt outstanding was $4,176.1 million as of September 30, 2026 , a decrease of $293.8 million compared to 

 the prior year, driven by a $100.0 million accelerated debt repayment in January 2026 resulting in the full 

 3 

 redemption of the senior secured notes due November 2026, as well as the retirement of $192.6 million aggregate 

 principal of the senior secured notes due 2028 and senior notes due 2029 through a series of debt repurchases at 

 an approximate 5% discount to par. 

 Net cash provided by operating activities including discontinued operations for the first nine months of 2026 was 

 $399.0 million compared to $468.6 million in the prior year period. Free cash flow including discontinued 

 operations for the first nine months of 2026 was $233.1 million compared to $276.1 million in the prior year 

 period. 

 On October 6, 2026 the company completed the sale of the LS&H segment for net cash proceeds of $488.4 

 million .

 Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures 

 This release contains financial measures that have not been prepared in accordance with U.S. generally accepted 

 accounting principles (“GAAP”), including Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, Adjusted net income, 

 Adjusted diluted EPS, and Free cash flow. Non-GAAP financial measures are not recognized terms under GAAP, 

 are not measures of financial condition or liquidity, and should not be considered as an alternative to profit or loss 

 for the period determined in accordance with GAAP or operating cash flows determined in accordance with 

 4 

 GAAP. As a result, you should not consider such measures in isolation from, or as a substitute for, financial 

 measures or results of operations calculated or determined in accordance with GAAP. 

 We use non-GAAP measures internally in our operational and financial decision-making, to assess the operating 

 performance of our business, to assess performance for employee compensation purposes, and to decide how to 

 allocate resources. We believe that such measures allow us to focus on what we deem to be more reliable 

 indicators of ongoing operating performance and our ability to generate cash flow from operations, and we also 

 believe that investors may find these non-GAAP financial measures useful for the same reasons. Non-GAAP 

 measures are frequently used by securities analysts, investors, and other interested parties in their evaluation of 

 companies comparable to us, many of which present non-GAAP measures when reporting their results. Further, 

 these measures can be useful in evaluating our performance against our peer companies because we believe they 

 provide users with valuable insight into key components of our GAAP financial disclosure. However, non-GAAP 

 measures have limitations as analytical tools and because not all companies use identical calculations, our 

 presentation of non-GAAP financial measures may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other 

 companies. 

 Definitions and reconciliations of non-GAAP measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measures are 

 provided within the schedules attached to this release. Our presentation of non-GAAP measures should not be 

 construed as an inference that our future results will be unaffected by any of the adjusted items, or that any 

 projections and estimates will be realized in their entirety or at all.

 Reconciliations to Certain Non-GAAP Measures 

 Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin 

 Adjusted EBITDA represents Net income (loss) before the Provision (benefit) for income taxes , Depreciation and 

 amortization , and Interest expense, net , adjusted to exclude share-based compensation, impairments, restructuring 

 expenses, the impact of certain non-cash fair value adjustments on financial instruments, acquisition and/or 

 disposal-related transaction costs, unrealized foreign currency gains/losses, legal settlements, and other items that

 currency gains/losses, legal settlements, and other items that are included in net income (loss) for the period that

 adjustments.

 dilutive.
