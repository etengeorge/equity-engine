 KRAS Wild-Type Subgroup Achieved 43.3% ORR Versus

 POSITILITY, a First-In-Class Biomarker-Enriched

 PHILADELPHIA, Pennsylvania, October 29, 2026 — Iovance
Biotherapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: IOVA), a commercial biotechnology company developing and delivering tumor infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL)
therapies for cancer, today announced updated results from Cohorts 1 and 2 of its Phase 2 IOV-LUN-202 trial and a first-in-class biomarker-enriched
precision TIL strategy for lifileucel in previously treated non-squamous mNSCLC.

IOV-LUN-202 Cohorts 1 and 2 are completing enrollment and support the
planned sBLA for lifileucel in non-squamous mNSCLC in 2027.

As of the October 14, 2026 data cutoff, 17 of 69 patients responded,
representing an ORR of 24.6%, consistent with the 25.6% ORR previously reported in 39 patients.

A retrospective analysis identified a biomarker-enriched subgroup defined
by Kirsten rat sarcoma viral oncogene homolog ( KRAS ) wild-type status, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 ( HER2 ) wild-type
status, and current or former smoking. This subgroup represented less than one-third of the broader study population (22 of 69 patients)
but accounted for more than three-quarters of the observed responses (13 of 17), with an ORR of 59.1% (13 of 22 patients).

The KRAS findings and further biomarker enrichment provide the
clinical foundation for Iovance’s precision TIL strategy. Iovance will prospectively evaluate this novel approach with POSI TIL ITY,
a first-in-class, prospective, biomarker-enriched cohort within the Phase 2 IOV-LUN-202 trial, designed with registrational intent.

 Updated Results in the Broader Non-Squamous mNSCLC Population 

Updated results from Cohorts 1 and 2 include 30 additional patients,
bringing the total to 69 patients with non-squamous mNSCLC whose disease had progressed on or after platinum-based chemotherapy and immune
checkpoint inhibitor therapy. Patients were enrolled without prospective selection using the novel biomarker-enrichment criteria.

The 17 reported responses included 15 confirmed responses and two unconfirmed
partial responses (uPRs) awaiting confirmation. Radiologic assessments by an independent review committee are ongoing.

Response durability and the safety profile remained consistent with
previous reports. The study incorporates an updated, reduced-dose lymphodepletion regimen that has improved the overall safety profile.

The broader IOV-LUN-202 Cohorts 1 and 2 are completing enrollment,
with approximately 80 non-squamous NSCLC patients expected in the final dataset, and an sBLA for lifileucel in non-squamous mNSCLC is
planned for 2027. Discussions with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) are ongoing, and Iovance expects to provide further updates
on the broader-population program in 2027.

 KRAS Wild-Type Status Anchors the Precision TIL Strategy 

To understand differences in response, Iovance evaluated clinical outcomes
alongside tumor genomic and translational data. The analysis focused on tumor antigen presentation—the process by which tumor cells
display targets for recognition by polyclonal TIL—and immune activity within the tumor microenvironment. Published preclinical studies
in lung cancer models have linked oncogenic KRAS signaling to impaired antigen presentation and immune suppression, providing a
biological rationale for investigating the association between KRAS status and response to lifileucel. 1 

 KRAS status emerged as the lead biomarker for Iovance’s
novel enrichment strategy. In the broader population of 69 patients with non-squamous mNSCLC, the ORR was 43.3% (13 of 30 patients) in
the KRAS wild-type subgroup, compared with 11.4% (4 of 35 patients) in the KRAS -mutant subgroup. Both complete responses
occurred in patients with KRAS wild-type tumors. These findings provided the rationale for further enrichment within the KRAS 
wild-type subgroup.

Building on this signal, Iovance incorporated HER2 wild-type
status and current or former smoking status into its enrichment strategy. These criteria draw on standard tumor genomic testing and routinely
collected smoking history. Preclinical research suggests that aberrant HER2 signaling can suppress antitumor immunity, while smoking-associated
mutations generate additional tumor targets for recognition by polyclonal TIL.

All 13 responses in the KRAS wild-type subgroup occurred among
the 22 patients meeting the combined enrichment criteria, yielding an ORR of 59.1%, including both complete responses. These patients
represented 31.9% of the broader study population but accounted for 76.5% of the observed responses. Together, the clinical findings and
biological rationale support prospective evaluation of this combined enrichment strategy for lifileucel. The results for these populations

“These findings are reshaping how we develop TIL therapy for
mNSCLC,” said Frederick Vogt, Ph.D., J.D., Interim President and Chief Executive Officer of Iovance. “The expanded dataset
reinforces the activity of lifileucel in previously treated disease, while the concentration of responses in KRAS wild-type tumors
points to a more precision approach to patient selection. In the enriched subgroup, nearly six in ten patients responded, including two
complete responses. We believe these insights could expand lifileucel’s potential as a one-time treatment for mNSCLC, with a best-in-class
profile compared to other therapies in development.”

Iovance has launched POSI TIL ITY as a first-in-class, prospective,
biomarker-enriched cohort within the ongoing IOV-LUN-202 program, with enrollment to begin in November 2026 at the first of over 70 existing
IOV-LUN-202 sites. Iovance intends for the study to support a potential registrational pathway, subject to FDA feedback.

“Tumor-reactive T cells need recognizable targets and a tumor
microenvironment that supports a sustained antitumor response,” said Wayne Rothbaum, co-Chair of the Scientific Committee of Iovance’s
Board of Directors. “The response pattern in IOV-LUN-202 provided a clinical rationale for testing how tumor biology can guide TIL
therapy. Starting with KRAS status, and refining selection through HER2 status and smoking history, we identified a highly
responsive mNSCLC subgroup where TIL biology converges with an immuno-reactive tumor microenvironment. We now aim to translate these novel
findings into a precision development plan utilizing KRAS -based stratification, designed to identify patients most likely to benefit
from lifileucel therapy.”

 Supporting Frontline Data and Development Plans 

In a separate analysis of frontline Cohort 3A of IOV-COM-202, six of
seven patients with non-squamous mNSCLC, KRAS wild-type tumors, and a current or former smoking history responded to lifileucel
plus pembrolizumab (without chemotherapy), yielding an ORR of 85.7%. HER2 status was not assessed in these patients. These findings
provide additional rationale for prospective evaluation of lifileucel in the frontline setting.

Iovance plans to initiate OP TIL ISM, an innovative, frontline
trial in biomarker-enriched non-squamous mNSCLC, in 2027. OP TIL ISM and the second-line POSI TIL ITY trial are intended to
evaluate lifileucel across two treatment settings and assess the role of biomarker-guided patient selection within the mNSCLC development
program.

Across the patient populations enrolling into OP TIL ISM and POSI TIL ITY,
the prevalence of the biomarker-enriched population is estimated at approximately 45% of the cases of non-squamous mNSCLC diagnosed each
year in the U.S., leading to more than 90,000 annual deaths and representing a significant market opportunity for lifileucel.

Additional details on the clinical results and development plans are
available in a presentation posted to the Investor Relations section of www.iovance.com.

 About IOV-LUN-202 

IOV-LUN-202 is a Phase 2 study evaluating lifileucel in patients with
previously treated mNSCLC. This update reports results from the 69 patients in Cohorts 1 and 2 with non-squamous histology who received
a total viable cell count of at least 10 billion. Lifileucel remains investigational for the treatment of mNSCLC.

 About Iovance Biotherapeutics 

Iovance Biotherapeutics, Inc. is the global leader in innovating, developing,
and delivering tumor infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) cell therapies for patients with solid tumors. Amtagvi® (lifileucel) is the first
FDA-approved, one-time treatment for previously treated advanced melanoma, now approved in three global markets and available at nearly
100 authorized treatment centers (ATCs). The Iovance TIL platform spans registrational trials and next-generation programs in additional
solid tumors, including gene-edited and IL-12 tethered TIL therapies, next-generation IL-2, and precision immuno-oncology approaches.
As the first and only company to take TIL therapy from concept to a broadly accessible commercial treatment, Iovance operates as an end-to-end
cell therapy company, anchored by fully owned, centralized U.S.-based manufacturing that is scaled to serve thousands of cancer patients
worldwide each year. Headquartered in Pennsylvania with offices and laboratories in California and Florida, Iovance serves patients at
ATCs and clinical sites across almost the entire U.S. and in many countries around the world. For more information, please visit www.iovance.com.
