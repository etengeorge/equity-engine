Non-GAAP Financial Measures To supplement Alta’s consolidated financial statements, which are prepared in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”), this presentation includes certain non-GAAP financial measures, including Adjusted EBITDA, Unlevered Cash Flow, Free Cash Flow before RTS Decisioning, net debt, net leverage, Tangible Invested Capital and ROTIC. Alta believes these measures may assist investors in comparing performance over various reporting periods on a consistent basis by removing the impact of items that do not reflect core operating performance, are used by Alta’s management to assess its performance, and are useful in evaluating operating performance and cash generation. These measures are not calculated in accordance with GAAP and should not be considered a substitute for, or in isolation from, net income (loss), revenues, operating profit, debt or any other measure calculated in accordance with GAAP, and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. Other companies may calculate Adjusted EBITDA, Unlevered Cash Flow, Free Cash Flow before RTS Decisioning, net debt, net leverage, Tangible Invested Capital, ROTIC and other non-GAAP measures differently, and therefore Alta’s non-GAAP financial measures may not be directly comparable. Alta defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest expense (not including floor plan interest paid on new equipment), income taxes, and depreciation and amortization, adjusted for certain items that management believes are not indicative of underlying operating performance. Net debt and net leverage exclude floor plan notes payable on new equipment, which management views as interest-bearing accounts payable repaid when the related equipment is sold. Items excluded from these measures, such as cost of capital, tax structure and the historic cost of depreciable assets, are significant components in understanding financial performance. For definitions and, where applicable, reconciliations to the most comparable GAAP measures, see the Appendix to this presentation. See the Appendix for company-specific adjustments and definitions applicable to JGP. Unlevered Cash Flow is a non-GAAP measure intended to illustrate cash generation before financing activities and working-capital movements. For Alta, Unlevered Cash Flow is defined as Free Cash Flow before RTS Decisioning, as presented in Alta’s Quarterly Earnings Presentations; for JGP, Unlevered Cash Flow is defined as Adjusted EBITDA less estimated rental-fleet maintenance capex, estimated non-rental capex, and cash taxes. Unlevered Cash Flow is intended to illustrate cash generation ability on a consistent basis within the presentation. JGP figures reflect normalized estimates of rental-fleet maintenance and non-rental capital expenditures. Tangible Invested Capital is defined as total equity plus debt and finance lease obligations (including line of credit) plus used and rental equipment floorplan debt, less cash, goodwill and other intangible assets, net; only floor plan financing on new equipment and operating lease liabilities are excluded; combined Tangible Invested Capital is the sum of standalone amounts, before acquisition purchase-accounting adjustments. ROTIC (Return on Tangible Invested Capital) is calculated as Unlevered Cash Flow divided by Tangible Invested Capital.
